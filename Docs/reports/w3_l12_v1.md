## 0. One paragraph

scene12 is imageless and rides **G3** (primary) + **G9** (secondary). Reading G3 at full
resolution changed the plan twice. First, the deck's timber was bound to the `wood_dark` map

## 2. GT-44 — the deck timber, and how the target was arrived at

### 2.1 The defect, in the scene's own instrument

`M["deckwood"]` bound the `wood_dark` map with **no tint**. Re-measured at full resolution this
lane (an independent reproduction of `w3_s10_rebuild_v1.md` §2.2's figure, not a copy):

```
wood_dark_diff.jpg   mean linear (0.0824, 0.0584, 0.0442) · Y 0.0625 · L* 30.03 · a* +3.76 · b* +7.48
```

and the scene's own `albedo_selfcheck` printed **알베도 0.081** for it. The band is
`L* 53–60 · a* 0…+2 · b* +4…+10 · albedo 0.22–0.28`.

### 2.2 The band's clauses disagree at the floor — stated, not smoothed

`L* 53 ↔ Y 0.2105`, which is **below** the albedo clause's 0.22. So the four clauses do not
describe the same set, and the **effective intersection is L\* 54.1–59.9**. Every target below is
inside that intersection, which is a stricter statement than "inside the band".

### 2.3 The reference read, and why it is an upper bound

**G9's boardwalk in diffuse light measures L\* 58.05 · a\* +1.41 · b\* +2.34** `[ref]`. That is
inside the band on L\*, but it is **a rendered pixel under sky illumination, i.e. an upper bound
on albedo, not an albedo** — so the target belongs below it, not at it. (G9's sunlit runs measure
L\* 65–69 with b\* +15…+20, which is the sun, not the timber.)

### 2.4 Declared at L\* 56.0, corrected once by the reference to L\* 55.0

L\* 56.0 was declared, built and **rendered first** (`260731_w3_l12`). Against the reference it
read **a shade bleached** — nearer driftwood grey than a 2–5 year patina — and its near-ground
band measured **mean 185** where **scene10's landed deck, the same product in the same library
under the same 49.79° L0 sun, measures 181**. Corrected **once**, which is the procedure S10
§2.2 records for exactly this situation. GT-44 was **amended in the ledger before the corrected
value landed** (`03d807c`), so the deviation is declared, not discovered in a diff.

| | declared | **landed** |
|---|---|---|
| target | L\* 56.0 · a\* +1.0 · b\* +6.0 | **L\* 55.0 · a\* +1.0 · b\* +6.0** |
| linear | (0.2642, 0.2355, 0.2012) | **(0.2537, 0.2258, 0.1925)** |
| albedo Y | 0.2391 | **0.2293** |
| `tint = target/source` | (3.207, 4.030, 4.553) | **(3.080, 3.864, 4.355)** |
| clipped texels | 0.0122 % | **0.0070 %** |
| `albedo_selfcheck` | 0.260 → pred 180.7 | **0.250 → pred 177.4** (CAP 222) |

**The reference moved the value within the standard; it did not overrule it.** Because clipping
is 0.007 %, the shipped map carries the target and **no weathered-plank procurement was opened** —
S10's G5 / §8.R OQ-8 test (*"only if that visibly fails"*) is not met and G5 stays unspent.

A separate darker soffit albedo was **refused with the arithmetic**: the effective band is only
5.8 L\* wide, so a deck/soffit split inside it is invisible. The top-face-vs-vertical-face
difference G9 and G10 both show is carried by **shading**, which the renderer already does.

## 3. GT-43 — the guard, and the three things deliberately not done

| member | before | **after** | basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| post | Ø64 round, painted steel | **Ø120 round 방부원형목재** | `s3_research_numbers_v1.md` §A5, KFS-TRAIL 그림 3-20 p.72, grade **A** |
| top rail | Ø70 | **Ø80** | same |
| mid rail | Ø40 | **Ø80** | same |
| stubs | Ø64 | **Ø120** | follow the post |
| material | `rail_color (0.30,0.31,0.33)`, metallic 0.25, `Looks/Rail` → class **metal** | **착색방부목**, `wood_dark` × derived tint, `Looks/DeckGuardWood` → class **wood** | G3 |

The guard tint is derived from **five clean G3 patches** measured this session (L\* 32.7 shadowed
post … 51.6 sunlit bench slat, a\* +0.5…+3.8, b\* +6.8…+20.7 — patches with negative a\* were
discarded as grass-contaminated). Target **L\* 44.0 · a\* +3.0 · b\* +12.0** → linear
(0.1766, 0.1320, 0.0894), albedo **0.1384**, tint **(2.144, 2.258, 2.022)**, clipping 0.0001 %.
Research §D5 supports treating this as a **different product**, not the same timber at another
age: 착색방부목 starts brown instead of following the ACQ green → honey → driftwood-grey path.

**Consequence, measured rather than hoped**: deck↔guard luminance contrast becomes **1.73×**,
where the outgoing painted steel against the *corrected* deck would have been only **1.29×**. The
railing cue reads **better** after the swap, not worse — which was the one real risk in the row.

### 3.1 Refused, each with its reason

* **Balusters — refused.** `w3_s10_rebuild_v1.md` §2's square sawn vocabulary (90×90 newel,
  38×140 rail, 38×38 baluster @0.150) is a **park-deck 방부각재** product; G3's river-edge
  product is a round-log post-and-rail with no infill. Decisively: the h0.3 sight line running
  out **under the mid rail** *is* this scene's negative-obstacle premise, so infilling the bay
  would silently move the research variable — the §7.2 S03 fence-decline and S17-F4 precedent.
* **Post pitch — held at 1.5 m** against §A5's 1.2 m 통나무펜스 정간. The **2.4 m destroyed span
  is two bays of the 1.5 m grid** and the stubs at −5.4/−4.2 sit on it; re-pitching moves the
  hazard itself. Divergence declared.
* **Rail height — held at 1.05 m** (**L12-F1**). It is below both 조경설계기준 16.13.2(2)
  (산책로 1,100) and 16.20.2(2) (관찰데크 1,200), and scene10 landed 1.10 on the KNPS
  n=1,227 built-reality median. This scene's declared identity is a *degraded* guard, so moving
  the rail height without a ruling would move a cue. Routed, not taken.

## 4. The river — measured, not widened

The intake is explicit that *"12 and 17 must not be re-widened independently of 03"*. New
`river_view_selfcheck()` (pure projective geometry, GPU 0) reports the effective channel as
**y 3.70…70.00 = 66.3 m**, already **~2× scene03's landed 33.8 m**, so R03-1's inheritance is
satisfied by construction and **the water plane is untouched**.

Two instrument defects of my own were found and fixed before the numbers were used: the `bare=`
count was matching **its own source line**, and the vertical-subtense probe was **degenerate** on
this scene (scene03 measures on its sight axis because its river spans the axis its cameras look
down; scene12's river spans +Y while every preset looks +X). The subtense is now the
centre-column occupancy of the same sample, which is not axis-dependent.

As `w3_s03_v1.md` §1.2 established, R03-1's literal *"≥ 1/3 of the frame width"* is satisfied
trivially by any horizontal band and is **not** reported as a pass.

## 7. Found on my own pilot, and fixed rather than reported around

Raising the deck to albedo 0.229 exposed that scene12 bound its three soiling roles to **rock
maps**: `stain_dirt = M["gravel"]`, `stain_water = silt = M["wetrock"]` (the riprap map at 0.65
scale). Dark-on-dark they read as damp patches; on a corrected deck they read as **loose gravel
and wet cobbles lying on the planks**, filling a third of the near band at `preset_h0.3_d5`
(`_w3_l12_crops/scene12_preset_h0.3_d5_ab.png` shows it plainly).

ground_kit's own **R3** rule is that the decal ladder must be a **tone separator, never relief or
another material** — and a different material entirely is the strongest possible violation of it.
This is the defect scene20 fixed at `c30b65c` and scene01's pilot before it. Rebound to the
deck's **own** map at a fraction of the deck's own tone: dirt **0.62×** (albedo 0.142), water
**0.52×** + `wet_rough` (albedo 0.119), silt = the water tone. Material only, **0 prims**, hash
unchanged.

Isolated: `l12b → l12c` = FAIL 2 · WARN 6 · PASS 8. Note that this **removed** some apparent
"improvement" from the earlier numbers — `h0.3_d5`'s sd 64.3 → 30.7 and σ_LF 23.5 → 7.5 were
inflated by the wrong-material plates creating false contrast. §6.1's table is the honest one.
