## 2. Measured findings

### 2.1 L15-F4 — the house roofs were made of wood, in the class table and in the render

`_look_spec` resolves a material's role **from the prim path**. scene15 named its roof material
`Looks/Roof_{i}`, and `scene_common.LOOK_ROLE` carries:

```
# Misclassification fixes (2nd diagnosis): Roof is a temple timber tile roof, not metal, …
"Roof": "wood", "Roof_": "wood",
```

That entry was written for scene07's temple. scene15 inherited it silently. The consequence is not
cosmetic — `_promote_const_to_texture` then bound a real texture `[measured this session]`:

| roof tint | authored colour | promoted to | base_color | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tint 0 (orange) | (0.55, 0.31, 0.22) | **`wood_dark_diff.jpg`** | **(6.842, 5.424, 5.073)** | dark timber planks at a >5× gain |
| tint 1 (grey) | (0.42, 0.42, 0.44) | *promotion refused* | (0.42, 0.42, 0.44) | flat constant colour |

So one roof family shipped a plank texture and the other shipped a flat plate, on the **same** roof
form, in the same alley. The pre-arm crop shows both plainly (`l15_roof_beauty.png`, left panel).

**Fix, scene-side only** (the class table is in a frozen kit): the path becomes `Looks/Slab_{i}`,
which resolves to class **concrete** and promotes to `concrete_floor` with the authored mean
preserved `[measured]`. The tints become the two finishes that actually cover Korean hillside-village
roofs — **녹색 우레탄 방수 (0.115, 0.185, 0.125)** and weathered cement **(0.255, 0.250, 0.240)**.

**A second, independent reason the old values were wrong**: the grey roof's Rec.709 albedo was
**0.4215**, and the orange roof's **0.3545**. `ground_kit.ALBEDO_CAP` is **0.30**. Both roofs were
above the library's own albedo ceiling, which is why they blew out — `white %` in
`beauty_overview` was **15.28 %** before and is **1.96 %** after (§9).

### 2.2 L15-F5 — the alley floor shipped a texture mean the stair beside it never saw

`M["alley"]` binds `concrete_floor` **directly**. `M["stair"]` binds a *constant colour* and is
therefore routed through promotion, whose whole job is to re-normalise the texture so the authored
albedo survives. Same texture, two routes, two very different results `[measured, `_texture_mean`]`:

```
concrete_floor_diff.jpg linear mean = (0.146453, 0.110206, 0.073338)     R/B = 2.00
  stair  (constant 0.20,0.20,0.19) → promoted, base_color (1.366,1.815,2.591) → mean = the authored grey
  alley  (bound directly)          → no re-normalisation                → mean = the raw warm brown
```

That is the tan/burlap floor in every h0.3 crop of every round this scene has ever shipped, next to a
neutral-grey stair cut from the same texture.

**Fix**: a **luminance-preserving** tint. Rec.709 linear Y is held at **0.115250 → 0.115253**
(Δ = +3.2e-6) and only the hue moves, onto the scene's own declared concrete ratio **1 : 1 : 0.95**
(`stair_color`, fix B-15-1) — target mean (0.115668, 0.115668, 0.109885), tint = target / mean =
**(0.7898, 1.0496, 1.4983)**. A tint folds into `base_color` as one multiply
(`scene_common._make_ground_pbr`, the fatal-C1 note), so this is exactly one albedo multiply.

**Why the arithmetic is internal and not a pixel read off G2**: G2's ground is **wet granite under
rain**, and a specular wet surface is an upper bound on albedo, not an albedo (the same reasoning
GT-44 used to sit *below* its G9 reference read rather than at it). Reading a number off it would be
a fabricated measurement. The scene already declares its own cement value and the stair already
renders it; matching the floor to it is both defensible and photometrically null.

**Why photometric nullity matters here and not only as an elegance**: the alley/stair material break
is `cue_material_break`, an **ablation cue**. Holding Y exactly means the cue's luminance contrast is
unchanged by construction — the correction moves hue and nothing else, and the h0.3 judging band
confirms it (§9: h0.3 means move −0.5 / +3.4 / −0.2).

Crop: `_w3_l15_crops/l15_floor_h03_d5.png`.

### 2.3 L15-F1 — a 20 mm facade interpenetration the file's own comment denied

`PARAMS["pots"]` carried the comment *"y=±0.40 (stair half-width 0.6, pot r 0.20 → never reaches the
facade at 0.58)"*. **0.40 + 0.20 = 0.600.** Every `|y| = 0.40` container was **20 mm inside** House[4]

That is the green blob in every v5.1+ crop. It is gone with the sphere (§3); the material survives
only as `ground_kit`'s weed **fallback** for a machine with no vegetation pack.

### 2.5 L15-F3 (pre-existing, NOT fixed, reported) — the window frames float

At `h1.8_d2` and `narrow_up` the window/door frames read as separate plates hovering off the wall at

### 2.6 Two kit defects found while measuring, handed over, not fixed

Both are in `scene_common.py`, which this window's rules freeze to K-micro:

- **L15-K1 — `LOOK_ROLE["Pot_"]` is dead code.** `_look_spec` strips trailing digits **and
  underscores** (`name.rstrip("0123456789_")`), so a prim named `Pot_0` reduces to `Pot`, and the
  table's key is `Pot_`. The lookup can never hit. Every `Pot_*` material in the library has been
  resolving to `misc` (verified: `_look_spec("/W/Looks/Pot_0") → ("misc", …)`), which is the *minimal*
  prescription — no texture, no detail normal. Same defect shape as S08-F2.
- **L15-K2 — `LOOK_ROLE` has a duplicate key.** `"Panel"` appears twice in the same dict literal,
  first as `"sign"` and later as `"metal"`; the later silently wins. Harmless today, but it means one
  of the two intents in that file is unreachable and no reader can tell which.

**Widths, not heights, are the binding constraint**, and `place_shrubs` jitters scale by ±8 %, so each
`target_h` is solved from the **worst-case** width `[computed]`:
`Grass_Short_C` w_max = 0.304 × (0.125/0.125) × 1.08 = **0.328** ≤ 0.360 budget ·
`Cedar_Shrub` w_max = 0.288 × (0.45/0.876) × 1.08 = **0.160** ≤ 0.360.

**Two divergences from `w3r_prop_mapping_v1.md` §B2d, both stated with the arithmetic:**

1. **The Ø0.45 고무 대야 does not fit.** A site at |y| = 0.40 facing a facade at 0.58 leaves 0.180 m.
   The stocked Ø0.35 size is used instead.
2. **The library holds no vegetable**, and B2d's *"planted with 고추 / 상추 / 화초"* cannot be built
   literally. `Grass_Short_C` is the only tuft whose native aspect gives a ~0.33 m clump at a 0.13 m
   height — the silhouette of a 상추 box — and it is used **as a stated substitution**, not as a claim
   that lettuce was modelled. The 화분/대야 conifers need no substitution note: a narrow evergreen in a
   pot (향나무·측백) is a literal Korean alley plant.
   The re-adjudication's *"FLIP container → `planter_pot_clay`"* (`w3_asset_readjudication_v1.md` §6.1)
   is **declined with a reason**: a 266 mm CC0 clay pot is one Western container type, and B2d's own
   headline is *"Korean-ness is the entire point of this prop"* — a 스티로폼 박스 and a 고무 대야 exist
   in no CC0 pack and are the two containers that actually make a Korean alley read. Procurement is
   therefore not opened, and 0 urban_kit call sites are added (so the T4b `treatment=` rule has no
   subject in this scene).

### 3.2 G-4 — the manhole, re-derived from the sewer

The intake names 15 as *"the near-window pilot's origin"*, and the file admits it in its own comment:
the site was chosen because *"x = −2.40 gives X = 2.60 m · 414 px = 21.6 % at d5"*. Every term in
that sentence is a camera term. **That comment is preserved verbatim in the file** so the retirement
is auditable rather than silent.

**New derivation** — KDS 61 40 00: a chamber is required at 방향 / 경사 / 관경 변화 or a **합류**, and a
Ø ≤ 600 straight run goes **75 m** between chambers. A 12 m alley therefore owes **zero** interval
manholes and exactly **one junction**: House[0] (cx −2.6, north) and House[1] (cx −2.6, south) put
their 오수 branches into the main at the same station. So the chamber goes to **x = −2.60**, on the
main's own alignment — the alley centreline **y = 0.00**, not 0.15 m off it. The 우수 측구 keeps its
separate line at y = −0.75.

The camera arithmetic survives **as a check**: d2 behind the eye · d5 X = 2.40 m → **23.4 %** ·
d10 **7.6 %** — inside the ≤ 25 % band the old value was designed *around*. Clearances re-measured
and gated: U gutter **+301 mm** · wall grime band **+426 mm** · patch #1 **+1433 mm** · 시공줄눈 JX at
x = −3.000 **+76 mm**.

## 4. Era doctrine, and what RF-1 can and cannot mean here

`era_consistency_survey_v1.md` §2.1 dates this scene **1960s–80s, informal** — *"the corpus's canonical
ABSENT-REAL scene"*, and §4.1 already blessed the bare default. §4.4 mismatch **1** is the open item,
and it names this scene:

> *"scene15's `cue_railing=True` variant is described as the compliant version. For a 1960s–80s alley
> it should be described and built as the **retrofit** version. And the retrofit is brand new, not
> weathered … at the corpus's present day the alley-stair handrail is either **absent** or **days
> old**: bright unweathered stainless on fresh base plates against 1970s concrete."*

**Executed**: the pipe was painted mild steel gone chalky (metallic 0.20, rough 0.72) — an object as
old as the wall it is bolted to, which erases exactly the contrast the variant exists to carry. It is
now **STS304**, §5.1's 2000s–2010s retrofit row: metallic **0.9**, roughness **0.35** (mill/brushed,
*not* mirror — §5.1 warns against reading that value as polish). The material path also moves
`Looks/Pipe` → `Looks/Handrail`, because "Pipe" resolved to class **misc** (no prescription at all)
while "Handrail" resolves to **metal** `[measured]`. The docstring and `SCENE_CONFIG` comments that
called this the *compliant* version now call it the retrofit.

**RF-1 base plates — refused rather than faked.** The brief asks for *"RF-1 retrofit plates where any
post exists"*. **scene15 has no ground post**: the poles were deleted in v5.1 and the rail is
wall-bracketed by design (§15(3), a both-sides-walled stair takes a handrail, not a guard). The
bracket-side plate would have to be authored inside `stair_kit.build_handrail`, a frozen kit this
window. Filed to the kit lane in §11; nothing was invented scene-side to look like compliance.

## 5. Season pin (§7 ruling 8) and the element audit

**G18 = summer** — full green shrub mass, dry golden sand, high sun, blue sky. This is scene18's own
pin (`w3_s18_v1.md` §5), and imageless scenes inherit their nearest image's season, so **scene15 =
summer**. Every element audited:

| element | audit |
|---|---|
| pot plants | `edge_weed` → `Grass_Short_C`, `border_narrow` → `Cedar_Shrub`. Neither carries bloom or autumn colour, so **no seasonal strip is needed** (the K4-F1 pixel rule, not a name heuristic). **Asserted by the gate**: the reachable species set is intersected with `{Rhododendron, Forsythia, Burning_Bush}` and must be empty |
| retaining-wall hedge | `sc.build_hedge` — clipped evergreen band, season-neutral |
| ground | `alley_concrete` profile: joints · manhole · U gutter · 2 patches · grime band · cracks · weeds. **No `leaf_ground` role and no fall-leaf scatter anywhere** — verified in the profile and in the scene's `M2` map |
| trees | **there are none, and there should be none.** scene15 has **0 `build_tree` call sites** and **no `SCENE_SPECIES` row** — a 1.2 m inter-building alley has no street tree. The species flip the brief asks for therefore has no subject here; that is the correct answer, not a skipped one |
| sky | `qwantani_noon_puresky` — cloudless, where G18 has scattered cumulus. **Same deviation S18 declared and for the same reason**: swapping the sky moves `noon_sun_elev` / `hdri_sun_rotz_offset` / `SUN_AZ_OFFSET`, all pinned by `variation_kit`'s azimuth ledger. That is a lighting work package, not this one. The **season** (summer noon, high sun) is correct as it stands |
| roofs | 녹색 우레탄 방수 / 시멘트 — season-neutral |
