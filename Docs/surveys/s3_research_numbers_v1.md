> "(2) 계단의 폭은 연결도로의 폭과 같거나 그 이상의 폭으로, **단높이는 18cm 이하, 단너비는 26cm 이상**으로 한다.

> (4) 높이 1m를 초과하는 계단으로서 계단 양측에 벽, 기타 이와 유사한 것이 없는 경우에는 난간을 두고, 계단의 폭이 3m를 초과하면 매 3m 이내마다 난간을 설치한다. 다만, **계단의 단높이가 15cm 이하이고 단너비가 30cm 이상일 경우에는 예외**로 할 수 있다.
> (5) **옥외에 설치하는 계단의 단수는 최소 2단 이상**으로 하며 계단바닥은 미끄러움을 방지할 수 있는 구조로 설계한다."
>
> **Verbatim (KCS 34 50 10, 3.2.8(3)):** "계단 및 경사로의 규격은 관련 법규에 적합하여야 하며, 이때 단 높이(R)와 너비(T)는 **2R+T=60~65 cm**를 유지하되, **전 구간에 걸쳐 동일하여야 하고**, 미끄러지지 않도록 표면 처리하여야 한다."
>
> **Verbatim (KFS-TRAIL p.70):** "계단높이는 **15cm이하**, 노폭은 **25~30cm**이면 일반적으로 적당하며, 계단높이(H), 노폭(B)과의 관계는 **2H+B=60~70cm** 정도로 한다."

**BUILDABLE PICK for a steep deck stair: R = 170 mm, T = 300 mm.** This one pair satisfies *every* source simultaneously:
- 조경설계기준 5.10.2(2): R ≤ 180 ✓, T ≥ 260 ✓
- KCS 34 50 10 3.2.8(3): 2R+T = **640** mm, inside 600–650 ✓
- KFS-TRAIL p.70: 2H+B inside 600–700 ✓
- KFS-TRAIL 표 13-1 30° row: exactly 170/300 ✓

Fallback pair R = 150 / T = 300 → 2R+T = 600, also inside every window, **and** it trips the 조경설계기준 5.10.2(4) railing exemption (R ≤150 **and** T ≥300) — useful if you want a hand-rail-free flight.

**PRIOR ASSUMPTION VERDICT — CONFIRMED.** rise 150–180 / tread ≥280–300 sits inside the legal envelope on every axis. Two refinements:
- **rise upper bound:** 조경설계기준 caps landscape stairs at **180 mm**, but KFS 표 13-1 (the worked design example in the same manual) specifies **200 mm at 35°**. So 150–180 is right for a *park* deck; a *steep trail* deck may legitimately reach **200 mm**. Use 150–180 by default.
- **the two KFS tables disagree:** 표 3-6 (p.71, general guidance) caps H at 150 mm; 표 13-1 (p.166, the as-designed spec) uses 200 @35° and 170 @30°. Prefer 표 13-1 for build numbers — it is the one the 특별시방서 declares binding on "**이하에 기술되는 모든 계단공종**".

**Verdict — ANSWERS "is it 1.2 m?": YES, but only because it is a DECK.** The authority chain resolves cleanly:

1. A trail/park deck stair is **not a 건축물**, so **PIRAN-15 (85 cm handrail, 3 m landing) and HOUSE-18 (1.2 m, 주택단지) do NOT bind it.** 산업안전보건기준 (0.9–1.2 m) is temporary-works only and also does not bind it.
2. The binding document is **조경설계기준 (→ KDS 34)**, and it sets **two different numbers**: a plain 산책로/휴게소 **안전난간 is ≥110 cm**, while a **데크 is ≥120 cm** (16.20.2(2)).
3. **The built data agrees with both.** KNPS-RAIL median = **1.10 m**; the top three modes are **1.00 (36%), 1.20 (20%), 1.10 (15%)** — i.e. the population straddles exactly the 110/120 pair the standard prescribes, with a 1.00 m tail from rope/wire trail railings that predate or sidestep the standard.

→ **MODEL AT 1,200 mm** for a 방부목 deck stair (it is a 데크, so 16.20.2(2) applies, and 1.20 m is the second-strongest built mode). Use 1,100 mm if the flight is a plain ground-level 산책로 step run with no deck structure. 1.0 m is common in the field but is the rope-railing archetype, not the timber-deck one.

> "높이 **2m**를 넘는 계단에는 **2m 이내마다** 당해 계단의 **유효폭 이상**의 폭으로 **너비 120cm 이상**인 참을 둔다."

→ **Use 2 m, not 3 m**, as the landing pitch for a park/trail deck stair. At R = 170 mm that is **a landing every 11–12 risers**; at R = 150 mm, every 13 risers.

**Switchback landing size — now sourced, not assumed:** **1,500 × 1,500 mm.** 조경설계기준 5.9(4) specifies exactly "1.5m × 1.5m 이상의 수평면으로 된 참" for continuous ramps; 5.10.2(3) requires stair landings to be ≥ the stair's own clear width, which for the modal 1.5 m deck stair is 1,500 mm. The two independently converge on 1,500 × 1,500. Confidence **A/B**.

**Real-world caveat:** the KFS BOQ (§A2b) specifies a **22단 deck stair as one 개소 with no landing line-item** — 3.3–4.4 m of rise uninterrupted, i.e. roughly double what 5.10.2(3) allows. Built trail stairs frequently ignore the landing rule on steep ground. If the scene wants *realism* over *compliance*, a 15–25-riser unbroken flight is the honest choice; if it wants a designed park look, insert landings every ~2 m of rise.

### B00c. Moss — **there IS a Korean design-standard clause. Grade A.**

### B5. Nosing / 미끄럼방지 on stone stairs — **clause VERIFIED verbatim; one real fix to the in-repo quote**
*(section order in §B is B00 → B0 → B5 → B1–B4b, reflecting the order evidence arrived; B1–B4b below carry the temple-specific heritage-spec numbers.)*

Read verbatim from the official 별표 PDF the repo itself links: https://www.law.go.kr/flDownload.do?gubun=&flSeq=148118977&bylClsCd=110201 (23 pp, 개정 2024-12-26).

**The in-repo citation label is CORRECT** — the clause really does exist at 「장애인·노인·임산부 등의 편의증진 보장에 관한 법률」 시행규칙 [별표 1] <개정 2023-12-11> **제8호 마.(2)** (PDF: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/flDownload.do?flSeq=162950009&bylClsCd=110201). *(An earlier draft of this note wrongly flagged the label as an error; that was my mistake — I had opened the repo's **second** linked PDF, flSeq=148118977, which is the parallel 교통약자법 별표1 where the identical sentence sits at 아.5)나). Both laws carry it, exactly as the repo's own §2.1 table says.)*

**The ONE real correction — the in-repo quote drops the proviso, and the proviso is the whole answer.** Verbatim (identical in both laws):

> **나) 계단코에는 줄눈넣기를 하거나 경질고무류 등의 미끄럼방지재로 마감하여야 한다. 다만, 바닥표면 전체를 미끄러지지 아니하는 재질로 마감한 경우에는 그러하지 아니하다.**

**Verdict on "does 자연석 계단 need a nosing strip?" — NO. Three independent grounds, in strength order:**
1. **The 단서 exempts it directly.** A riven natural-stone tread *is* "바닥표면 전체를 미끄러지지 아니하는 재질로 마감한" — and KFS-TRAIL 특별시방서 13-2 나 says so in as many words: "**거친 면을 발판으로 미끄러짐을 방지한다**". The non-slip duty is discharged by the stone itself; no brass/rubber strip required. **conf A.**
2. **Scope.** 별표1 is issued under 시행규칙 제2조제1항 and reaches only *편의시설* at 대상시설 listed under 법 제7조 + 시행령 별표1. A pilgrimage stone stairway on temple grounds / a mountain path is outside it entirely (it would only bite if the flight were the designated barrier-free 접근로 of a 종교집회장 above the floor-area threshold). **conf B.**
3. **Statutory relaxation.** Even in scope, **법 제15조 (편의시설 설치기준 적용의 완화)** lets the 시설주 obtain 시설주관기관 approval to relax the standard where compliance would damage "「문화유산의 보존 및 활용에 관한 법률」에 따른 문화유산 … 으로서의 역사적인 가치". **conf B** — law.go.kr full text is JS/403-gated to fetchers; confirmed via 법제처 index + 보건복지부 처리지침 references, not read verbatim.

**Positive counterpart in the heritage spec — what stone stairs DO instead of a nosing:**
> 2024 국가유산수리 표준시방서 **0400-3.1 ㄷ** (p.90): "**디딤돌은 보행시 미끄러짐 등이 발생하지 않도록 가공한다**"

**Verdict — TREAD CORRECTED, RISE NARROWED.** Prior guess rise 150–200 / tread 300–450:
- **rise → 120–180 mm, mode 150 mm** for anything built to a modern standard.
- **tread → 260–350 mm** on 25–35° ground. Treads >350 mm only appear on gentle slopes (410 mm at 20°, 560 mm at 15° per KFS 표 3-6). **The 450 mm upper guess is wrong for a temple approach**, which is steep by definition.
- **Historic flights are steeper than any modern standard.** Bulguksa's stair runs at 45° — i.e. rise ≈ tread. If the scene wants a *genuine* temple stair rather than a restored/park one, allow **rise up to ~200–240 mm with tread ≈ rise**, and treat the modern 150/300 band as the "recently rebuilt" look.

**Cross-check on width:** 조경설계기준 21.8(5) says 900–1,100 (1-person) / ~1,300 (2-person); the KNPS built stone-stair median is **1,200 mm** — the two agree. But a *temple approach* is a ceremonial axis: Bulguksa ≈ 5.1 m. Pick by scene role, not by the trail number.

### B4. Rise irregularity — **no Korean measured survey exists. Documented [assumed] distribution below.**

Searched: KCI, RISS-indexed titles, 전통조경학회지 / 건축역사연구 / 한국산림휴양학회, 국가유산 지식이음 발간자료, 수리보고서 listings. **No published Korean survey reports riser-height variance or std-dev for temple / traditional stone stairs.** The heritage spec deliberately sets **no tolerance** — it says only "일정한 높이의 단으로 선을 맞춰 쌓도록 한다" (0400-3.2 ㄱ) while *explicitly permitting* field adjustment by chipping stone, wedging 잔돌, and digging. The one quantitative uniformity rule in Korean law (디딤판 너비/챌면 높이 "균일하게", 편의증진법 별표1) does not reach heritage stairs.

**Closest measured analogue (real, citable):** Harper et al., *Interactive Journal of Medical Research*, **2025** — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11754982/ — 2 engineered public stairways, 48 steps / 4 flights: mean riser **168 mm (SD 4 mm)**; within-flight max−min **5–14 mm**; code tolerance **9.5 mm (3/8 in)**; 80 % of fall events occurred on the flights with 14 mm riser and 38 mm depth variation. **conf A** (measured, but not Korean and not heritage).

**[assumed] distributions for procedural generation.** Reasoning: engineered stairs held to a 9.5 mm legal tolerance still measure SD 4 mm / range 5–14 mm. Heritage stone stairs have **no** tolerance, are hand-fitted by breaking and wedging, and sit on a settling earth core with only ≥50 mm interlock → scale variance up ~2–3× (dressed) and ~5–7× (rubble). **conf C.**

| flight type | rise | tread | per-flight max−min rise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **마름돌 / 장대석 (dressed)** | N(μ=**170**, σ=**10**) mm, truncated ±2.5σ | N(320, σ=15) mm | **25–40 mm** |
| **거친돌 / 막돌 (rubble natural stone)** | N(μ=**160**, σ=**25**) mm, truncated ±2.5σ | N(300, σ=**50**) mm | **70–110 mm** |

Plus, for both: per-step out-of-level roll/pitch **U(−2.5°, +2.5°)**; a deliberate forward drainage pitch of **1–2 %**; and **long-wave settlement** — a low-frequency sinusoid of amplitude **±20 mm over 8–12 steps** so the error is *spatially correlated, not white*. Hand-laid stone on an earth core settles in patches, and white noise reads as fake.
*Rationale for tread σ ≫ rise σ:* KFS 표 3-6 re-selects the run B from local gradient (300 → 850 mm) while pinning H at 150 mm — run genuinely varies far more than rise.

> **KEY NEGATIVE FINDING (conf C — absence across 나무공장, 대림우드, 우드앤홈, 에이스목재, 아이피엔터):**
> **100×100, 105×105, 80×80, 50×100, 30×30 and 45×90 are NOT Korean 방부목 stock sizes.** Korean posts are metric-planed **90각 / 120각 / 140각**. The brief's guesses of "newel 90×90 or 81×81, rail 45×90, baluster 38×38/30×30" resolve as: **newel 90×90 ✔ (81×81 ✘), rail 45×45 or 38×140 (45×90 ✘), baluster 38×38 ✔ or 45×45 ✔ (30×30 ✘), decking 21×120 / 25×140 (38×140 is a joist, not a deck board).**
>
> **Tension to be aware of:** KFS-TRAIL's 2010 drawings specify **80×80 and 100×100 방부각재** (§A5). Those are *drawing* sizes from a government manual, not current retail stock. For a buildable 2026 spec use **90×90 / 120×120**; for faithfulness to the published KFS detail use 80×80 / 100×100. Flag whichever you pick.

**Buildable recipe (inference, conf C):** 데크판 **21×120** or **25×140** @3600 · 장선 **38×140** 세워시공 · 멍에 **90×90** · 난간기둥 **90×90** (120×120 at stair corners) · 난간대 **38×140** or 45×45 · 살대 **45×45** or **38×38** at ≤100 mm clear (≤150 mm mid-flight, §A4).

**CCA is NOT legal for Korean 방부목재.** conf **A**: CCA has **no row at all** in 표 1, and the standard's own footnote states `'-' : 해당 약제는 적합 기준이 없으므로 사용할 수 없음`. Corroborating (conf B): arsenic preservatives banned in 어린이활동공간 by 환경보건법 시행령 별표2; CCA barred from residential wood since 2007 (나무신문 https://www.imwood.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=21967); anything sold as "방부목재" must be **≥H3** since 2015-06-19 (한국목재신문 https://www.woodkorea.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=28975).
→ **Model it as ACQ (or CuAz) H3B above grade, H4 at ground contact. Never CCA.**
(Independently corroborated by SMCS-34 표 2.4-1, §A5, whose approved list also omits CCA.)

**The single most useful number:** copper preservative drops fresh L\* from ~73.5 to **~46** — then holds it nearly stable (ΔE 3.18 over 6 mo vs 18.95 untreated). ACQ wood starts *dark*, not bright.

**Derived photometry** (Y = ((L\*+16)/116)³ × 100; conf B, derived from A-grade L\*):

| surface | L\* | Y (visible diffuse reflectance) | sRGB neutral |
|---|---|---|---|
| fresh untreated pine (bright / typical) | 79.8 / 73.6 | 56 % / 46 % | 198 / 181 |
| **fresh ACQ (green-olive)** | **45.9** | **15 %** | 109 |
| 6 mo weathered | 56.0 | 24 % | 134 |
| 9 mo weathered | 61.5 | 30 % | 149 |
| 12 mo weathered | ~52 | 20 % | 124 |
| **spruce decking, 4 yr grey** | ~55 | **23 %** | 133 |

*Broadband albedo caveat (conf C):* the above is *visible* reflectance; wood is markedly more reflective in NIR, so solar albedo ≈ 1.2–1.5× → fresh pine ~0.50–0.60, weathered grey deck ~0.28–0.35. No peer-reviewed measured wood albedo was found; only a vendor SRI datum (35 new → 48 after 1 yr, dark hardwood deck, https://hdgbuildingmaterials.com/products/solar-reflectance-index-hdg-primer/, conf C).

**Timeline:** first perceptible change days–weeks · **bulk of L\*/chroma loss in the first 3 months** (conf A, Ghosh Fig.1) · grey clearly dominant ~**12 months** · uniform silver-grey patina **2–5 years**, horizontal decking fastest. ACQ colour path: **green/olive (copper) → honey/tan as copper oxidises → driftwood grey** (Koppers NatureWood https://www.kopperspc.com.au/naturewood/, conf B). CuAz and Korean 착색방부목 start greenish-brown/brown instead. Mechanism: UV 300–550 nm depolymerises lignin → quinones rain-leached → cellulose-rich surface scatters broadband (grey), plus blue-stain/melanin darkening; L\* oscillates seasonally (falls each winter, rises each summer).

### D5. Shader target for a Korean 등산로 deck stair (synthesis, conf C on the exact triplets, resting on A-grade measurements)

| age | L\* | a\* | b\* | albedo | sRGB approx |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fresh ACQ H3, day 0 | 46 ±4 | +3…+5 | +18…+22 | 0.15–0.18 | #6E6144 – #7A6B4E |
| 6–12 months | 52–56 | +5…+7 | +18…+25 | 0.20–0.24 | warm dull tan |
| **2–5 years — the target** | **53–60** | **0…+2** | **+4…+10** | **0.22–0.28** | **#86837C – #96938B** |

> **Do NOT paint fresh ACQ a saturated green.** Measured a\* is only *slightly positive* (+2 to +5) — the "green" read comes from low lightness plus moderate yellow, not from negative a\*.
> Add per-board L\* variance of **±5** (measured SDs 0.9–3.1 plus board-to-board), a brighter worn strip along the walking line, and darker fungal streaks at joint shadows and north-facing edges.
