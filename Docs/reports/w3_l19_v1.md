## 2. GT-50 — the K4(d) flip, and the proof

### 2.1 The defect, in millimetres

`build_arc_steps`'s box convention lays one Cube per sub-segment with a tangential
half-width of `r_out · sin(Δθ/2) · 1.03`. With `sector_deg 7.5 / seg 3` → Δθ = 2.5°:

```
half-chord            89.877 mm
angular overshoot per side = asin(89.877 / r) − 1.25°
    r 1.20   3.0453°   (arc  63.78 mm)     r 3.00   0.4668°   (arc 24.44 mm)
    r 1.50   2.1851°   (arc  57.21 mm)     r 3.50   0.2215°   (arc 13.53 mm)
    r 2.00   1.3257°   (arc  46.27 mm)     r 4.00   0.0375°   (arc  2.62 mm)
box corner radii  1.203361 / 4.001010   (design 1.200 / 4.000)
```

Adjacent treads therefore **overlapped**, and where they overlapped the higher one won
the top face. The walked surface sat **one riser too high** in a wedge at every step
boundary — widest at the inner radius, where a pedestrian's foot is closest to the newel.

### 2.2 The split proof

GT-6's acceptance test is *"a prim-hash / GT-delta diff … proven, not asserted"*.
`_arc_split_proof()` (new, in the scene's own `NEGOBS_SMOKE=1` run, **GPU 0, 0.6 s**)
samples the walked top face on a 57 × 3601 polar grid over r 1.10…4.10, az −4…94°,
evaluating **both conventions from the same `PARAMS`** and taking the max against every
other solid that bids for the same (x, y) — the L walls, the newel, the corner slab, the
threshold, the edge guard, both walkways.

```
표본 205257 · 상이 17019 (8.29 %) · new-void 0 · new-solid 0
  -183.333 mm  n=17019
단 하나의 계층 = 정확히 −1 라이저(183.333 mm) · 디딤면 z 이동 0 → OK
인계 경계 [3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11] · a0/a1 끝단 [] (L 벽에 매몰) · 카이트 경계 [] (라이저 0) → OK
```

Four things this establishes, each of which could have gone the other way:

1. **One stratum only.** Every differing sample is `−183.333 mm`, exactly one riser.
   **No tread's own z moves** — the ladder (−0.150 ×3 kite, then 9 × 183.333 mm to
   −1.800) is bit-identical and the **total drop 1.800 m is invariant**. What moved was
   *which azimuth belongs to which tread*, which is the defect `mesh=` exists to fix.
2. **The kite is the negative control.** Boundaries 1| and 2| contribute **0** samples.
   They are the merged kite landing, where the riser is 0.000 m — so a probe that were
   measuring anything other than the hand-over would have lit them up.
3. **The ends are clean, and that is proved rather than assumed.** GT-29 had to declare
   this term for scene06: its landing `a0` ray overshot ≤ 4.25° into open air, putting a
   phantom slab 3.07 m above the tread. Here the same overshoot at az 0° and 90° falls
   **inside `Wall_south` (y −1…0) and `Wall_west` (x −1…0), both `z_top` 3.5**, so it can
   never win a top face. The probe returns 0 samples at both ends.
4. **No drop edge is created or destroyed.** The outer arc retracts by **1.010 mm** (the
   box's corner bulge collapsing onto the true ray); the inner radius is unchanged at the
   mid-ray. Both an order below `GT_DELTA = 0.020`. The 1.8 m fall to the corner slab and
   the hazard arc are untouched.

### 2.3 Why this is declared as a real movement and not a micro

GT-6's own class is *"B (micro): top-face +2 mm on landings only"*. **183.333 mm is not a
micro.** GT-29 folded its own −192 mm hand-over stratum into a class-B row on the strength
of the +2 mm term; scene19 has no +2 mm term to hide behind, so GT-50 declares the
hand-over as what it is and states plainly that **R-2 is owed and was not taken**: the
per-riser nosing azimuth moves by up to **63.78 mm of arc at r_in**, above `GT_DELTA`, so
GT-29's below-threshold exemption does not transfer. §4 of the ledger records what was
actually run; the empty R-2 line is the row not being closed, per ledger §0-3.

### 2.4 The three non-walked families

`Parapet_i` · `Rail_i` · `Nosing_i` were flipped in the same commit. They are guards and
cues **above** the walking plane, so R-3 and not GT — but the parapet ring is the thing
the eye reads as "curved", and under the box convention each 7.5° arc stood **0.343°
proud of its neighbour at `r_in` 3.82**, i.e. a stepped ledge every 7.5° along a nominally
smooth curve. That is precisely the vocabulary G8 was routed here to supply. `Nosing_i` is
`cue_nosing`-gated and default OFF; it was flipped anyway so the ablation arm is not left
on the retired convention (GT-40's tactile-arc precedent).

### 3.4 19-4 — the patches become 우레탄 덧방, and one rectangle class dies

Two separate defects, both measured at HEAD:

**(a) Count.** The intake row calls the prescription *"the second-highest count in the
set, on a roof membrane … three of them on one small roof is not [real]"* — and the file
shipped **four**. A urethane deck fails where water stands and where it is walked, not in
four scattered spots. Reduced to **2**, and the survivors carry **two different causes**,
which is what makes them read as maintenance rather than decoration:
`(11.60, −0.60)` the ponding ring of drain 1, 1.10 m off the gully centre; `(8.00, 2.60)`
the maintenance walking line out of the rooftop core door.

**(b) Material — the one that actually mattered.** Both patch roles were bound to
`M["coating"]`, **the identical material as the deck**. A 2 mm proud plate in the same
green at the same roughness is not a repair, it is nothing: the only way it could ever
read was as a silhouette rectangle, which is exactly the vocabulary U-6 bans. And
`patch_cut` was bound to `gk_stain`, the **dark asphalt saw-cut line** — the "reads as
asphalt" failure the L19 brief names, sitting live in the file.

New `patch_coat` (hue held to 3 decimals, value **+18 %**, roughness **0.72 → 0.46**) and
`patch_lap` (the feathered coat edge). The logic is that a 덧방 is *the same product
applied again*: identical hue, fresher value, and markedly more sheen because it has not
chalked. Sheen is the only cue that survives a shadowed frame. Measured in the delivered
frame (`preset_h0.3_d5`): 덧방 **L 185.4** against adjacent deck **L 178.0** = a contrast
ratio of **1.041** — present, and nowhere near a stencilled white plate.

`build_patch_field(cutline=False)` is the kit default and `apply_ground` never overrides
it, so **no cut line is built at all**: the saw-cut vocabulary is absent by construction,
and `patch_cut` is bound only so a future `cutline=True` cannot silently fall back to the
dark stain.

**On U-6.** The pixel evidence is in `_w3_l19_crops/preset_h0.3_d5_patch_zoom.png`: PRE
carries a large hairline-outlined dark rectangle on the deck — the "이상한 사각형 무늬"
made literal — and L19 does not. What survives is a soft 4 %-value plate. It is still a
rectangle, and I am not going to pretend otherwise: `build_patch_field` can only emit
axis-aligned rectangles and the kit is frozen (**L19-F7**). It survives on evidence, not
by default — a 우레탄 덧방 is masked off with tape on site and **is** roughly rectangular,
which is a different fact from a decorative rectangle stencilled on a plaza.

## 4. The W2 brightness note — answered

The W2 fix batch left an open item: *"scene19 `mean`: the membrane albedo lift moves the
h0.3 median frame mean to 182 … an eyes call on overall brightness is owed"*
(`w2_fixbatch_v1.md:340`, owner **eyes**). The L19 brief asks for it to be re-checked
under the current tone regime. Three things had to be separated to answer it:

**1. It is not the frame mean.** The h0.3 whole-frame median is **147.6** at L19 (148.0 at
the baseline), and `blown` is **0.00 %** on all 16 cuts. The 182 is the
`near_ground_stats` **near-ground band** metric, and it is **still 182**:

| round | sd | mean | wht% | σ_LF | gate line |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `260730_w2d_fix` (baseline) | 20.1 | **182** | 12.3 | 1.43 | `sd 20.1<32 · mean 182>170 · wht% 12.3≥2 · σ_LF 1.43<5` |
| `260731_w3_l19_pre` (attribution arm) | 18.5 | **182** | 12.4 | 1.32 | same four |
| `260731_w3_l19` | 18.5 | **182** | 12.3 | 1.42 | same four |

**2. L19 does not move it.** sd 20.1 → 18.5 happened between the baseline and the
attribution arm, i.e. it is **other lanes' kit work, not mine**. My own delta is
sd 0.0 · mean 0 · wht% −0.1 · **σ_LF +0.10** — and σ_LF moves *toward* its gate, because
the 덧방 adds the low-frequency tonal structure the metric is asking for.

**3. The 182 is correct, and this is measurable rather than arguable.** In the delivered
`roof_context` frame:

| surface | declared albedo | measured linear Y | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| parapet vertical face, sunlit | **0.40** | 0.3946 | **0.99** |
| membrane deck, sunlit | **0.19** | 0.2789 | 1.47 |
| granite planter side | 0.078 (texture) | 0.0443 | 0.57 |

The parapet lands on its declared constant to within 1 %, so **the material pipeline is
calibrated**; the deck's 1.47× is the irradiance difference between a horizontal plane and
a vertical face at solar elevation 49.79° (a horizontal surface sees `sin 49.79° = 0.764`
of the beam **plus the full sky hemisphere**, a vertical face `cos 49.79° = 0.646` of the
beam and half the hemisphere). The deck is therefore **physically consistent with its own

## 6. L19-F1 — the corridor photometry, measured and handed up

This is the largest thing wrong with scene19 and this lane did not fix it.

**The measurement.** Across the whole round, on the four cuts that actually show the fan
winder:

| cut | mean | pixels ≤ 8 LSB | vs deck cuts |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `lower_lookup` | **20.1** | **17.2 %** | h0.3 deck cuts sit at **147.6** |
| `radial_nosing` | **28.5** | **13.2 %** | |
| `winder_mid` | **35.9** | **25.5 %** | |
| `entry_gate` | 64.3 | 4.7 % | |

A quarter of `winder_mid` is crushed below 8 LSB. For a project whose thesis is *RGB
context cues for invisible drop estimation*, the scene's hazard is being quantised away.

**The cause is physical and the scene already knew it.** The winder occupies azimuth
0–90° in the re-entrant corner of an L wall that is 3.5 m tall on its south (`y −1…0`) and
west (`x −1…0` ) faces. At Korean latitude the noon sun is in the south, so a north-east-
facing corner recess is in that wall's shadow all day. The v4 docstring recorded it in
2026-07-27 (*"the whole winder corridor lies in the shadow of the L wall … the corridor
cuts receive sky light only"*) and declared lighting out of scope for that instruction.
L19 puts numbers on it. **My edit makes it marginally worse**: `winder_mid` −1.14 LSB and
crushed +0.45 pp, `lower_lookup` crushed +1.55 pp, because the 1.10 m guard casts a deeper
shadow. That is declared, not buried.

**Three levers, ranked, none taken unilaterally.**

1. **L-wall material (cheapest, material-only, R-3).** `granite_dark` measures **linear-Y
   0.078** and is bound to both L walls and the newel. A real Korean 옥탑 PH is 시멘트
   뿜칠 or 도장 at **0.35–0.50**, so this is a realism defect in its own right *and* it is
   what kills the bounce in a corridor that receives only sky light. **Not taken** because
   the dark wall is a **declared scene design constant** — `[A-19(1)]`, *"step material =
   light `plaza_light` (contrasts with the `granite_dark` walls)"* — and overriding
   another author's stated design intent on my own initiative, in the same window as a
   GT-class geometry flip, is what the ledger discipline exists to prevent.
2. **L-wall height 3.5 → 2.7 m.** A real 옥탑 PH above a roof deck is 2.4–3.0 m, so 3.5 is
   already generous. This is geometry and inside my remit, but the wall is a hazard box
   and its height is the datum three judged cuts were aimed against.
3. **Sun azimuth (`SUN_AZ_OFFSET 171.5`).** Rotating the building on its site is a free
   design choice and S11 set the precedent that a lane's measured SUN choice stands. **Not
   taken** because it is a global photometry move that would turn this round's clean
   FRAME-only adjudication into a photometry delta and make GT-50's split proof harder to
   read, for a benefit that belongs to a lighting round.

**Recommendation**: (1), as a one-constant material row in a lighting/polish round, with
the step/wall contrast re-derived rather than assumed. Handed up on the **S06-F3**
precedent (*"camera-adjacent and was not taken unilaterally"*).
