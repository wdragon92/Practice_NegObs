## 3. Built — the G4 ground layer

Everything is placed at a **perpendicular offset from the walk centreline** (upper trail polyline →
stair spine → lower trail polyline), never at a constant `cy`. That single change is what removes the
"row beside a ruled band" read at source: the litter, the tufts and the handline all bend with the path.

| family | count | how |
|---|---|---|
| drift lobes | **77** | `ground_kit.build_blot` (CB-2 **DEC-1**), 1 Mesh each, single face, zero thickness, `n=22`, `rough=0.20`, `proud` 0.006 m, `z_fn=ground_z` so every vertex sits on the local grade. 4 offset bands (1.42 / 2.05 / 3.55 / 5.45 m) with jittered offset, radii and along-line spacing, 10 % dropout |
| leaf cards | **320** | real 3-D leaf assets (`scene_common.VEG_DEBRIS`), instanced, laid along the local grade. **34 %** are seeded on lobe rims — DEC-2's feather ring, re-implemented (see §3.1) |
| turf tufts | **14** | `Grass_Short_A/B/C`, in clumps of 2–3, weighted toward the small ones |
| bare trunks | **22** | authored, leaf-off, two ranks (near frame 2.1–3.6 m off the walk, second rank 6–8 m) |
| rope handline | **12 posts + 60 rope segments** | §4 |
| apron drifts | 10 | the old 4 leaf piles + 6 new, the **only** lobes allowed on a walked approach surface (§3.2) |

**3.1 Why the lobes are DEC-1 + a scene-local card placer, not DEC-2.** `build_carpet_mask` would give
the feather ring for free, but its ring is an **unmasked AABB scatter** around the mask bbox. 07's yard
has the walk outside that box; **04's walk runs straight through the litter band**, so the ring would
throw leaf cards onto the stair treads and the trail. `feather_clip` cannot express the exclusion
either — the walk is a meandering strip, not an AABB. So the mask is `build_blot` (the same DEC-1
primitive DEC-2 is built on) and the ring is re-implemented in the scene with the walk mask applied.

**3.2 The one place litter is allowed on a walked surface.** Ten authored lobes lie on the 3 × 6 m
`ConnectU` approach landing and the trail band. At `proud` 0.006 m they are **decals**, an order below
the 0.024 m GT-E1′ tolerance, and `ground_kit`'s own `wear_lane` / `edge_litter` decals already sit on
that same slab at +0.6–2 mm. **None touches a stair tread**: the nosing cut line is this scene's hazard
cue and it stays clean. Everything else is filtered by `walk_clear()`, which measures clearance to the
trail band (worst-case half width 0.99 m, `width_jit` included) and to the stair corridor (|y| ≤ 0.90).

**3.3 Season selector.** `build_tree(bare=)` alone cannot deliver a leaf-off frame and its own docstring
says so: it draws species from `VEG_TREES` by coordinate hash and only `Elm_Sapling` of the three
bare-capable assets is in that pool, so `bare=True` yields a *mixed* frame with the oaks still green.
scene04 therefore carries its own species table (`TREES04`) and calls `add_vegetation` itself — the
route that docstring prescribes. `Shumard_Oak` is **excluded, not relegated**: its leaves ride inside
MASH `PointInstancer`s that also carry the branches, so it cannot be stripped, and a green broadleaf at
34 m is still green. The one evergreen (far field only) is `Douglas_Fir` — `Yellow_Pine` / `White_Pine`
are **retired species** under spec §10.2 and `placement_lint` LINT-4b errors on them (measured, §8).

## 4. The rope-on-timber-post handline (K4(c) new prop) — and R04-1

### 4.1 Built form

| quantity | value | basis |
|---|---|---|
| post diameter | **Ø 0.120 m** round timber | `[assumed]`, above the **≥ 0.07 m 끝마구리** floor 「조경공사 표준시방서」(2003 / 2016) sets for a 통나무 post line `[law, adjacent form]` — the clause governs a 철조망 post line, the nearest codified 통나무 post rule I could source |
| post pitch | **1.446 m** realised (cap 1.75) | same clause caps 통나무 post spacing at **≤ 1.8 m** `[law, adjacent form]`. G4 reads ~2 m `[assumed]` — we sit inside the codified cap |
| exposed height | **0.95 m** | KNPS-RAIL, n = 243 rows whose name contains 로프 → median **1.00 m** (`Docs/surveys/s3_research_numbers_v1.md` §A3) `[data, grade B]`. Grade B because the column is `폭높이` and may mix width entries; 0.95 is that median minus the grade-B margin and matches G4's post-to-step proportion |
| rope diameter | **Ø 0.018 m** | `[assumed]` — 16–20 mm is the trail band |
| sag | 0.075 × span, 6 segments/span | `[assumed]`, matched to G4 by eye |
| offset from walk | **1.35 m** | outboard of the stair half width 0.90, the trim strip 1.15 and the trail half width 0.99 |
| reach | x −0.55 … 6.60 along the walk | §4.3 |

Rope-and-post is not an exotic choice: in KNPS-RAIL the name keyword 로프 appears **437** times against
목재 195 and 데크 32 — it is *the* most common built trail railing type.

### 4.2 R04-1 — dressing, not a guard. Asserted in construction, not in prose

The scene's registry print (`verge_selfcheck` §3, printed on every run and every self-check) carries:

* **no rigid rail, no infill, no toe board** — one Ø18 mm catenary rope per span, nothing else;
* it stands **1.35 m off the walk centreline**, so it neither narrows the walking band nor stands
  between the walker and the drop;
* **0 members cross the drop edge** (x 0.00–5.55, |y| ≤ 0.90);
* **it is built in both hazard arms.** `ground_z()` resolves per arm, so the `hazard_stairs=False`
  control gets the identical handline on its flat plane. *This* is the property R04-1 needs: a dressing
  element that appears only in the hazard arm **is** a label cue, whatever a report says about it. The
  old code built the verge only when the slope existed; the new ground layer does not repeat that.

⇒ **the negative-obstacle label is unchanged: T4 irregular-riser sleeper stair, drop 1.450 m.**

### 4.3 Reach, and the camera keep-out

The handline covers the stepped reach only. Two reasons, both stated rather than hidden: a 로프난간 is
installed at the steep/stepped reach, not along a flat park path; and extending it up the meandering
trail would stand posts **0.85–1.08 m in front of the d5/d10 preset eyes** (which run along y = 0 while
the trail wanders off it). That is the "new geometry swallows a preset camera" regression this project
has had four times. Measured minimum eye-to-post distance over all 13 cameras: **1.410 m** ≥ the 1.20 m
keep-out, checked on the CPU.

### 4.4 Lift-readiness (Lane 1 may take it)

`rope_posts()` / `rope_span_points()` / `build_handline()` couple to the scene only through the walk
polyline and `ground_z`, both already values. Lifting into `props_kit` is a signature change
(`build_rope_handline(line, ground_fn, spec)`) and no logic change. The one non-obvious piece is the
segment orientation: `add_cylinder` authors `[translate, rotateY, rotateX]`, which under USD's
row-vector convention applies **rotX → rotY → translate**, so a local +Z maps to
`(cos a·sin b, −sin a, cos a·cos b)`; inverting that gives the two angles used, one prim per segment,
no pivot group.

## 6. 04-B — gravel masked off the lawn onto the trail

`plan_ground`'s field request is one AABB and `scatter_debris` has no mask hook, so P11's gravel field
(region 38.4 m², cover 0.10, count 150) was landing stones on the **lawn** as much as on the trail. The
injected callback is now a scene-local wrapper that replaces the single call with one call per tile of
`trail_tiles()` — axis-aligned boxes **inscribed in the trail band** (an AABB of half-side `a` fits a
strip of half width `h` at bearing θ iff `a(|sin θ| + |cos θ|) ≤ h`), with `max_count` shared by area.

* tiles: **6**, maximum distance of any tile corner outside the trail band: **+0.000 m** → lawn stones **0**, provable on the CPU;
* instances 150 → **36**. Areal density on the trail is **unchanged** (the profile's cover 0.10 is applied per tile); the drop is the lawn stones leaving plus the inscribed-box conservatism — tiles cover the middle **71 %** of the trail width, i.e. the wear line, which is where a compacted 마사토 trail actually shows its stone;
* the wrapper declares `mtl` / `sink` / `scale_jitter` **explicitly** rather than swallowing them in `**kw`, because `ground_kit` decides whether to pass them by inspecting the callback's parameters — a bare `**kw` would have silently dropped the `gk_rock` material override and the F2 burial fraction.

**G-6 deposition scatter stays gated and is not implemented** (intake: "do not implement it").

## 7. Defect found in another lane's file — `build_tree(bare=)` does not survive instancing

**This is the most important finding of the WP and it is not in this lane's file.**

`scene_common.BARE_SUBPRIMS` + `build_tree(..., bare=True)` (commit `1346b70`) deactivates
`/Root/leaves` on the referencing prim and **then** calls `SetInstanceable(True)`. The commit is
careful about that order and documents the trap it avoids. But:

> **USD ignores prim opinions on descendants of an instance.** The shared prototype is composed from
> the reference alone, so the deactivation is accepted on the stage and **discarded in the prototype**.

Measured twice this session:

1. `usd-core 26.8`, minimal repro: after a successful `SetActive(False)` on `/A/Asset/leaves` and
   `SetInstanceable(True)` on `/A/Asset`, `prim.GetPrototype()`'s children are
   **`['Looks', 'trunk', 'leaves']`**.
2. Pilot round 1 (`look_check/scene04/260731_w3_s04b`): the scene log reported **37 deactivations, 0
   failures**, and **every one of the 37 trees rendered in full green leaf**.

The K4 commit could not have caught this: its acceptance test was a 33/33 prim-hash identity with the
feature **off**, and no scene had opted in — the branch had never been rendered.

**Fix used here (scene-local + additive, no edit to `scene_common`):** three wrapper layers,
`assets/veg_bare/{Gray_Birch,Elm_Sapling,Lombardy_Poplar}_bare.usda` (deliberately **outside** the gitignored `assets/vegetation/` tree: the wrapper holds no NVIDIA asset content — one relative reference and one `over` — so it is trackable while the geometry it points at stays untracked and procured by `assets/download_vegetation.py`), each referencing its
source asset and carrying `over "leaves" (active = false)`. The `over` then sits **above** the instance
boundary, so all instances share one prototype whose children are `['Looks', 'trunk']`, and instancing
is kept. Verified with usd-core: two instances share `/__Prototype_1`; bare zmax 3.2960 / 3.0424 /
13.4221 m against leafed 3.3294 / 3.0867 / 13.6709 m (98.9 / 98.6 / 98.2 %) — so the scene binds the
**bare** zmax as `native_h` and the K4 note's 0.8–1.8 % height shortfall does not apply either.

**Owed to Lane K4** (`scene_common.py` is not this lane's file): either adopt the wrapper-layer pattern
inside `build_tree`, or drop instancing on the bare path, or document `bare=` as ineffective. The
scene-side workaround is in `TREES04` with the full reasoning in the code comment.
