## 0. Layout

```
look_check/
  <scene>/<round>/            judgement grids, corpus members, anchors, baselines-of-record
  _experiments/
    t0_spike/<round>/         T0 spike tree (incl. its conc* throughput arms)
    spike_p1/<dir>/           P1 realism spikes + the sample-count/time budget probe
    diag/<dir>/               dead-pixel and per-scene diagnostics
    gates/<scene>/<round>/    gate probes and zoom crops
    twins/<scene>/<round>/    A/B twin arms
  _t0_spike -> _experiments/t0_spike            (symlink, load-bearing)
  spike_probe -> _experiments/spike_p1/spike_probe (symlink, spec-cited)
  logs/, *.log, spike_results.json              render journals, left at root
```

**Rule.** A round lives at the scene root **iff** it is a judgement grid, a corpus member, a published anchor, or a scene's baseline-of-record. Everything else — crops, gate probes, A/B arms, spikes, diagnostics — lives under `_experiments/`. Consequence: `ls -t <scene>/*/` now returns a *judgement* round for every scene (§5).

**Symlink policy.** Symlinks exist only for paths a *future* consumer resolves — `_t0_spike/` (the `t1_material_layer_spec_v1.md` §7 A/B baseline convention, notably `_t0_spike/c2_A_base/`) and `spike_probe/` (the RTX runtime dump cited by `lighting_camera_variation_spec_v1.md` §513). They are deliberately **root-level only**: a symlink inside a scene folder would carry a fresh mtime and re-break latest-round discovery. Past-evidence paths were not symlinked — use the relocation map in §6.

Status legend — **baseline-of-record**: what the next regression run compares against · `corpus`: member of a `regression_check` v2.1 validation corpus (`w2_tools_v1.md` §4.3) · `anchor`: reproduces a published measurement anchor · `active`: W2 work in flight · `evidence`: cited by a report, no gate role.

> **Never rename, move or delete a row marked baseline-of-record / corpus / anchor.** Those exact
> paths are hard-coded in report reproduction commands and in the corpus driver.
