## 2. The bowl (GT-37)

### 2.1 The plan, and why the numbers are what they are

Bowl centre **C = (14.0, 0.0)**, rim radius **14.0**, floor **z = −4.500**, arena disc
**r ≤ 4.10**. `CX = R_RIM` is not decoration: it puts the near rim on the walk axis at **x = 0**,
exactly where the v6 rectangle put it, which is the one quantity the rebuild deliberately did
**not** move — the h0.3 grid is defined against it and the near-window arithmetic re-derives to the
same three windows because of it (§4.1).

| sector | span | content | courses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **cascade** | 150…210° | granite cascade, on the walk axis | 15 + **1.20 m mid landing** + 14, riser **0.150** / tread **0.300**; 30 risers = 4.500 |
| **tier** | 90…150° | timber seating tiers | 11 courses, riser 0.375 / tread 0.900; the 12th riser is the floor |
| **arcade** | 210…270° | forecourt r 4.10 → 17.0 under a ring deck, 6 columns at r 14.60, wood-slat soffit, 9 shopfront bays | clear height **3.70 m** |
| **ctrl** | 270…450° | plain curved retaining wall r 13.4 → 14.0 | — |

Both banks land on the same arena radius by construction, and the smoke run asserts it rather than
trusting it: `11 × 0.900 = 9.900 = R_RIM − R_ARENA` and `12 × 0.375 = 4.500 = the depth`.

Every plate in the scene is a **K4(d) true annular-sector mesh** (`build_arc_steps(seg=1,
mesh=True, arc_seg=N)` / `_annular_sector_mesh`). One mesh per course, faceted `arc_seg` times,
neighbouring courses sharing their rays exactly — so the box convention's 1.03 chord cover, and the
wedge gaps it exists to hide, are not reduced here but **unnecessary**.

### 2.2 The sector angles are a judgment constraint, not a style choice

This is the hardest thing in the rebuild and it is worth stating plainly. At h0.3 the graze cone
from `(−d, 0, 0.3)` over the near rim falls **0.15 m per metre**. Any *stepped* surface inside the
bowl that is simultaneously (a) above `z = −0.15 x` and (b) inside the 30° half-frame
(60° hFOV: 18.147 mm focal / 20.955 mm aperture) **announces the depth of the pit**. A plain
vertical wall may be in frame and above the cone — a wall reads as continuous ground; a stepped
bank does not.

The first plan put the tier bank at 70…150°. Scanned at 1° × 0.10 m that gives **369 points** that
are both above the cone and in frame — the outer courses at `a ≈ 70°`, `r ≈ 8.6`, top −2.25 against
a cone at −2.59. Moving the bank to **90…150° gives 0**. The scan is now a smoke gate
(`보울 내부 보행면 — 프레임 안 노출 0`), and a second gate covers the *standing* elements the
surface scan cannot see — flank railings, bed kerbs, shrub and tree crowns, arena benches, arcade
columns, shopfront heads, the facility sign. That second gate is what moved the in-bowl planting
bed from `a0 = 216°` to **222°**: its 5.4 m Fraxinus crown measured **29.5° bearing, 1.28 m above
the cone**, i.e. visible.

Concealment, re-derived: `x_hit = 4.500 · d / 0.3` = **30.0 / 75.0 / 150.0 m** at d = 2 / 5 / 10,
against a far rim at **28.0 m** — margin **+2.0 m** at the binding distance d = 2.

## 3. The guard — S08-B closed as B-1 (GT-38), and GT-7 folded

### 3.1 What was built

`props_kit.build_glass_balustrade` (the K4(c) G8 template) is used **verbatim** over **210…450°**:
0.30 m parapet upstand + coping, then shoe channel · 19 mm laminated panels in **1.35 m** bays with
a 12 mm joint · capping rail. **44 panels · 22 caps · 22 shoes**, total guard height **1.40 m** —
above the current statutory 1.1, where v6 was a sub-code 1.0. The "sub-code reality" moved off the
guard (G8 shows a modern glass system) and onto the fact that an opening exists at all.

Over **150…210° there is no guard whatsoever**: that is the cascade head, and a grand cascade has no
guard across its head in any real plaza, because the head *is* the way in. The tier-bank head
(90…150°) is likewise unguarded — a 0.375 m first riser is not a fall edge — and is closed with a
planting bed instead.

**The v5 deliberate deviation survives the rebuild, and B-1 is what pays for it.** The opening must
sit on the grid axis (+X, y = 0) or 6 of the 9 grid shots fill with parapet wall (the scene19 d5
blackout precedent). v6 bought that with a *demolished* run; v7 buys it with a legitimate stair
head.

### 3.2 S08-F1 — a finding against the kit, reported not fixed (kits are frozen)

`props_kit.build_glass_balustrade` **cannot be called on a non-X-aligned polyline**. It authors each
panel with `sc.add_box(..., (seg_len, panel_t, panel_h))` and each cap with
`sc.add_cylinder(..., rotY=90.0)` — both on **world** axes — so for a chord at bearing θ ≠ 0 the
panel's long axis and the cap's axis stay on +X. Its own `_selfcheck` only ever exercises
`[(0,0), (6,0)]`. The same limitation applies to `build_tube_railing`.

Client-side workaround used here, and it is the correct one: each chord gets its own
`sc.build_rot_group(pivot = chord midpoint, rot = chord bearing)` and the kit runs in that local
frame. **The kit is used verbatim; nothing was re-implemented.** The 22-chord segmentation over 240°
leaves a 6.4 cm sagitta per chord — real curved balustrades are faceted, so this is the right shape
as well as the cheap one. **Owed to the K-track**: a `bearing=` / `rotz=` kwarg on both builders.

### 3.3 GT-7, and the supersede-note discipline

GT-7 (tempbar deletion, `OPEN`, CB-8) is **folded into GT-38** rather than landed separately,
because its declared class (`R-3` + `R-1`) is strictly contained in GT-37's full re-cache and the
ledger's §0-2 allows one re-cache per scene per batch. The fold is conditional and the condition was
met: `w3_execution_spec_v1.md` §5.2's CB-8 line requires *"the supersede note in the message"*, and
the §1.6 note — which overrides `tonglam_v2.md` §1 row 08's PASS — is reproduced **verbatim** in the
geometry commit `0af6f24`. GT-7's §3 row now carries a note saying exactly this, and says that
without the note the fold is not permitted.

Deleted, and gated in the smoke run: `build_tempbar`, `PARAMS["tempbar"]`, the 2 `TempPost_*`
collision boxes, the 3 tempbar materials, the tempbar block of the self-check.

**Consequence to carry forward** (spec §1.6): scene08's 통람 PASS is not carried forward. It enters
통람 v3 unranked.
