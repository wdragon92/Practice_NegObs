## 1. The paving

### 1.1 What the ruling settles, and what it does not

§7.2 supersedes the 07-29 ruling on **one clause**. That ruling said two things — *"03 stays natural, the
cycle track holds only for the scene17 levee"*. The image doctrine overturns **(a) natural surface**.
**(b) no cycle track is untouched**: this crest still has no bike road, no lane marking and no centre line,
and D6's deleted centre line stays deleted. Anyone reading the paving as a licence to put the cycle track
back would be reading a clause that was never lifted.

### 1.3 Why 4.0 m + 0.4 m, and why the band is on the **landward** margin

**Width.** A Hangang 제방/둔치 산책로 runs 4–6 m. 4.0 m is the low end of that band and is the widest this
crest can carry while leaving the **1.000 m grass shoulder** (x −1.000…0.000) between the paving and an
*unguarded* 3.2 m drop. The river-side paving edge therefore stays at **x = −1.000, exactly where the gravel
road's edge was** — so the GT-E2 argument the v7 code already carries for that seam (5.65 rows @1080 at d10
against a 16-row floor) transfers verbatim, and **no new drop-parallel line enters the near window**.

**The band position is a declared divergence from G3.** G3 puts its light-grey block band on the **river**
side, in the bench strip. Here that would put a light/dark line **1.0–1.4 m in front of the drop edge and
0.6–1.0 m in front of the `h0.3_d2` judged eye, running parallel to the drop** — i.e. it would act as a
**drop cue the scene never declared**. `SCENE_CONFIG`'s `cue_*` flags are this scene's cue vector; adding an
undeclared one silently moves the research variable. On the landward margin the band is 4.0–4.4 m from the
drop and cues nothing. The band itself — the thing G3 actually shows — is kept; only its side moves.

### 1.4 `proud` stays 0.0015, and the reason is the stair, in millimetres

scene17's paved crest uses `proud = 0.006`, so 0.006 was the obvious value. It is wrong here.
`_stair_steps` starts the flight at `z_top = 0.000`, so **the first riser measured from the walked surface
is `0.160 + proud`**:

| proud | first riser | irregularity against 19 uniform 0.160 risers |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0015 (held)** | **0.1615 m** | **+0.94 %** |
| 0.006 (scene17's crown) | 0.1660 m | +3.75 % |

편의증진법 별표1 requires uniform 챌면. And 0.006 buys nothing visually: the landward paving edge is
**behind** the d5 eye (x = −5.0 vs the edge at −5.4) and **4.6 m ahead** of the d10 eye, where a 4.5 mm step
subtends ~0.02 px. So the drop edge at x = 0 keeps **3.2015 m** exactly, and GT-46's z-profile claim is
"no existing walked face moves", not a hope.

### 1.5 +76 prims, fully attributed

`river_band` emits one rot group **and** one slope box per seg, and `river_segments()` returns **19** segs.

```
removed  LeveeRoad   width 3.0, max_w 3.5 -> 1 sub-band x 19 segs x 2 =  38
added    CrestWalk   width 4.0, max_w 3.5 -> 2 sub-bands x 19 x 2     =  76
added    CrestBand   width 0.4, max_w 2.5 -> 1 sub-band  x 19 x 2     =  38
                                                            net       = +76
```

`max_w` stays **3.5**, the crest hard band's existing convention: it splits the 4.0 m field into 2 × 2.0 m
sub-bands, i.e. two narrow rotation interfaces rather than one wide one, which is the trade `river_band`'s
own docstring prescribes at a material border.

## 2. The consequences of paving (GT-47)

### 2.1 The ground plan

* **`pave.module` `(None, None)` → `(0.200, 0.100)`** — truthful now that the crest *is* laid in blocks;
  `unit_cell` was always 0.200 in the kit ledger and `_assert_unit_cell`'s U1 check passes. **Measured
  no-op in prims**: the only readers of `pave["module"]` are `build_patch_field` and `build_relaid_units`,
  and this scene builds neither.
* **`pave.joint` stays `None` — declined with coordinates, not with taste.** `joint="interlock"` composes
  `build_joint_grid` from the profile's `step_x = 3.0` as **x = const grooves spanning the whole y range**,
  i.e. lines **parallel to the drop edge at x = 0**. Measured sites: **x = −12.000 / −9.000 / −6.000 /
  −3.000** on the old region, **x = −3.000** on the paved band after `_edge_guard_ticks` — and x = −3.000
  lands on the **d10 GT-E2 E-band boundary** ([0.7d, 2.2d] = x −3.000…+12.000). The archetype is also
  wrong: flexible block paving has no 3 m 시공줄눈; its joints are the **3.5 mm sand joints between every
  unit** (`joint_interlock_w`, `ground_kit.py:250`), which the texture delivers geometrically free — see §2.2.
* **region `x0` −12.000 → −5.400** (the paved band's outer edge). CPU A/B on `plan_ground`, both arms:
  **prims 17 → 17, elements 17 → 17**, so it costs and buys nothing in count. What it buys is placement:
  with the 12 m region **4 of the 8 `stain` decals landed at x ≤ −6.254**, i.e. on mown grass, floating
  1.5 mm above turf. **Correction to GT-47's §3 text, which said "every stain lands on paving":** with the
  new region **1 of 8** still has its centre at x < −5.000 — the lobe at x[−5.358, −4.812], which *straddles*
  the band's outer edge. That is not the defect the change was aimed at (a dirt stain lapping onto turf at
  a paving edge is what one does), but the row's wording was absolute and the measurement is not, so it is
  corrected here and in the §4 landing record rather than left to be discovered.
* **edge break x −4.000 → −5.400** (the seam that is now the hard/soft boundary). `break_y` **2.400
  unchanged** — the bound is on |dx(y)|, not on x: |dx(2.400)| = **0.0946 m** < the 0.100 m half-width
  (|dx(3.000)| = 0.1479 m, still off the seam). The river-side seam at x = −1.000 is still deliberately
  **not** broken, and its old reason is now stronger, not weaker: for |y| ≤ 1.2 it is **paving-on-paving**
  (the apron takes the same blocks), so there is no material boundary there at all.

### 2.2 The block pattern comes from the texture, and the number is the ledger's own

`scene_common.py:158-163` records that the PavingStones015 map carries 12 repeats per tile, so a block's
long side is 0.17 / 0.20 / 0.25 m at `scale_m` 1.0 / 1.2 / 1.5. **`scale_m = 1.2` is exactly the 인터로킹
200 × 100 standard** — the same module `GROUND_DIMENSIONS["unit_cell"]["levee_paved"]` declares. So the
declared module, the rendered module and the kit ledger agree, and no geometric joint prim is needed to
say so.

### 2.3 Ablation-arm integrity — a live defect the paving exposed

This is the finding of this section, not a tidy-up. Two arms were bound to `M["gravel"]` **because the crest
was gravel**, and would have silently become cue-bearing against a paved crest:

| arm | what it is for | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cue_material_break = False` | the arm that must have **no** crest↔stair material break | stair `gravel` — matched the gravel crest | stair takes **the crest paving**, so the binding follows the crest and cannot drift again |
| `hazard_stairs = False` (`build_flat_fill`) | the twin that must show **no line at x = 0** where the drop was | fill `gravel` — matched the gravel apron over the whole hazard corridor (\|y\| ≤ 0.95 ⊂ apron \|y\| ≤ 1.2) | fill takes the crest paving, so the invariant survives |

Left as it was, GT-46 would have converted the negative control into a second cue-on arm without anything
in the pipeline noticing — the judge channel never renders these arms.

### 2.4 P03-F2 — the stains, found on the pilot's first pass

| element | binding | rendered sRGB | the paving beside it | reads as |

### 2.6 `wear` re-bound

## 3. The tone item S03 handed on (`w3_s03_v1.md` §7-3) — closed at its cause

### 3.1 The cause, measured at the texture rather than argued

S03's residual was never a missing-element problem. Both maps carry almost the same mean albedo; they
differ entirely in their **highlight tail**:

| map | mean sRGB lum | sd | **p99** | **>204 %** | >224 % | linear albedo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gravel_diff` | 137.3 | 37.1 | **215.4** | **3.56** | 0.22 | 0.2855 |
| `paving_interlock_diff` | 141.1 | 18.1 | **173.6** | **0.09** | 0.01 | 0.2777 |

**Same mean albedo, 40× fewer near-white texels.** The near-white crest was the gravel scan's bright-stone
tail. Paving the crest removes it by construction, which is why this is a fix at the cause and not a
cosmetic swap.

### 3.2 `near_ground_stats` — the numbers the brief asked for

`preset_h0.3_d2`, band B45 (`sd`/`mean`/`p99`/`>224%`) and B30 (`wht%`/`σ_LF`):

| metric | HEAD twin (pre-S03) | **`260731_w3_s03`** (gravel) | **`260731_w3_p03`** (paved) | gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sd` | 23.8 | 22.6 | **25.8** | ≥ 32 (WARN) |
| `mean` | 172 | 194 | **178** | ≤ 170 |
| `p99` | — | 232 | **219** | report |
| `>224 %` | 0.87 | **5.06 (violating)** | **0.34** | ≤ 5 ✔ |
| `wht %` | 6.8 | **39.1** | **8.6** | < 2 |
| `σ_LF` | 4.34 | 0.94 | **3.11** | ≥ 5 (WARN) |
| `flat_gnd` | 7.5 | 5.9 | 6.0 | < 3 |

`preset_h0.3_d5` moves the other way and it is declared, not hidden: `mean` 126 → **173**, `wht%` 0.0 →
**6.4**, `σ_LF` 4.16 → **2.47**. The cause is not a defect — at d5 the near window (x −4.50…−3.30) used to
be **lawn** and is now **paving**, and a sunlit clay-block promenade is ~2.3× the reflectance of turf
(`grass_lawn` albedo 0.0932 vs paving 0.211). It is physics arriving with the archetype. `preset_h0.3_d10`
is unchanged to the digit (its near window is still turf).

### 3.3 Is any gravel left in a near window? No — measured

The brief's conditional (*"if any gravel remains, region-restrict the scatter"*) does not fire. The h0.3
near windows are `x −1.50…−0.30` (d2), `−4.50…−3.30` (d5), `−9.50…−8.30` (d10). The paved band spans
**x −5.400…−1.000** plus the apron **−1.000…0.000**; d2 and d5 are wholly inside it and d10 is turf. **No
`gravel`-bound prim falls in any h0.3 near window.** The region-restricted scatter S03 filed with the kit
lane is therefore **withdrawn as scene03's blocker**; it stays open for the kit's other natural-profile
clients (04 · 07 · 09 · 12 · D3 · 10), and this lane says so rather than letting a closed ask sit open
against its name.

### 3.4 The residual, re-attributed — and this is the part that is genuinely new

The paving is measured against **G3's own promenade, through the same instrument and at the pixel level**:

| | sRGB | luminance | sd | >204 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G3 promenade, sunlit near field** | 209.7 / 171.5 / 130.2 | **176.6** | 11.3 | **0.26** |
| G3 promenade, mid field | 214.7 / 178.9 / 140.5 | 183.8 | 7.0 | 0.02 |
| **p03 `h0.3_d2` near paving** | 196.9 / 178.7 / 153.7 | **180.8** | 21.9 | **8.50** |
| p03 `h0.3_d5` near paving | 195.1 / 177.1 / 152.1 | 179.1 | 20.7 | 6.41 |
| p03 `levee_walk` near paving | 195.6 / 177.3 / 152.0 | 179.3 | 17.0 | 4.20 |
| s03 `h0.3_d2` near **gravel** (before) | 208.5 / 192.1 / 170.1 | 194.0 | 24.3 | **38.87** |

Three readings, each with its own consequence:

1. **The luminance is right.** 179–181 against the reference's own **177–184**. The `mean ≤ 170` gate miss
   is *the reference photograph's miss too* — the gate is a 실사 n=10 maximum across all ground types, and
   a sunlit clay-block promenade at noon sits at the top of that distribution. **The tint is therefore not
   tuned down to pass the gate**, because doing so would take the render away from the measured reference
   in order to satisfy a statistic derived from other surfaces.
2. **The residual near-white is specular, not albedo — and here is the test that separates them.** Within
   the d2 near paving, the `>204` pixels are measurably **desaturated toward the illuminant**:

   | pixel set | n | sRGB | B/R | saturation `(max−min)/max` |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | all | 191,232 | 196.9 / 178.7 / 153.7 | 0.781 | 0.224 |
   | **> 204** | 16,257 | 222.7 / 210.8 / 191.6 | **0.860** | **0.140** |
   | ≤ 204 | 174,975 | 194.5 / 175.8 / 150.2 | 0.772 | 0.232 |

   A bright *albedo* keeps the material's chroma; a **specular/sheen** response takes the illuminant's.
   The bright tail does the latter. So the remaining 8.5 % is the block faces' specular response under the
   L0 noon sun (`paving_interlock_rough` + the MDL's `rough_noise_a 0.22`), **not** the diffuse tone. That
   is a T1/material question with a concrete handle — a strictly better hand-off than S03's *"no decal can
   fix an albedo"*.
3. **The chroma is short of the reference and the amount is stated.** Ours R:G:B = 1.000 / 0.907 / 0.781
   against G3's 1.000 / 0.818 / 0.621 — roughly **half the warm departure from neutral**. Part is the
   reference's warm global illuminant (its "neutral" concrete reads 193/161/115), part is the ground MDL's
   own `desat_bright_a = 0.30`. **Not chased by raising the tint**: fighting the look layer's desaturation
   from a scene file would push the red channel toward the albedo cap to buy a hue. Filed in §8-3.
4. **`σ_LF` 3.11 against the reference's 12.53.** G3's low-frequency structure at that distance is
   **bench, fence and tree shadows striping the promenade** plus the adjacent stone apron and grass. Our d2
   frame has no shadow-casting object in the near field at all. That is a **dressing** ask, not a material
   one, and it is nameable — §8-4. *(Read the 12.53 as directional: G3's B45 band mixes promenade, stone
   apron, grass and shadow, where our d2 band is one paved plane. It is an anchor, not a like-for-like
   target.)*
