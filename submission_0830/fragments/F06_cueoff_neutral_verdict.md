> The pre-registered primary rule returned **zero CUE EVIDENCE across 30 cells, of which only 12 were judgeable at all**. What the intervention established is narrower than "the model reads cues" and worse than it: **these detectors respond to whether the picture looks like the hazard scene, not to whether the hazard is in it.** Arm C's false-alarm rate is monotone in how far its surgery moved the picture (58.4 % → 1.000, 36.6 % → 1.000, 13.1 % → 0.361, 1.0 % → 0.042) and flat in cue inventory — the scene that keeps **every** cue noun is the one that fires least.
