# F7 — strict-H twin pixel-difference audit (RED-TEAM RESPONSE, D35 / R1-F7)

Generated by `rt_response/code/f7_hpair_pixdiff.py` (+ `f7b_noisefloor.py`, `fig_f7_panels.py`) · CPU · read-only over the frozen renders.

**The question.** strict-H is a *geometric* predicate — `int_px == 0 ∧ edge_vis == 0`, i.e. the hazard prism re-projects to zero pixels.  It does not forbid the hazard from changing the image through shadow, ambient occlusion, GI bounce, mesh seams or sub-threshold silhouettes.  R1-F7 asked what is actually left.  All 96 test H twin pairs were re-read at native 1920×1080 and differenced: `d = max_c |on_c − off_c|`.

**Pairing gate.** All 96 pairs verified pose-exact to 1e-6 on (d, h_rel, yaw, pitch, roll, hfov, ground_z) — consistent with `METRICS.md` N.1 (H: 96 EXACT / 0 TOL / 0 EXCL).


## 0. The threshold has to be calibrated first — the brief's 2/255 is renderer noise

`sceneN3`'s `keep_dressing` off arm is **geometrically identical** to its on arm (`ctrl_dressing/CTRL_TABLE.md`: heightmap `max |Δ| = 0.000000 m`, NaN pattern equal), so every pixel that differs between those two renders is path-tracer / OptiX-denoiser nondeterminism.  Measured over its 24 cuts:

| reference pair | n | median px ≥ 2/255 | median px ≥ 8/255 | **median px ≥ 32/255** | median mean-magnitude over changed px |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **renderer noise floor** — N3 on vs N3 keep-dressing off (identical geometry) | 24 | 916841 (44.2 % of frame) | 27571 | **50** | 3.19 |
| hazard-only — C2 on vs C2 keep-dressing off (drop deleted, dressing kept) | 24 | 1270241 | 875005 | 698078 | 61.87 |

**A 2/255 threshold flips 44 % of the frame on two renders of the same geometry.** Every statistic below is therefore reported at three levels, and **≥ 32/255 is the level that carries scene content** — its noise floor is 50 pixels out of 2 073 600.

**Verdict: strict-H is never optically empty in this corpus.** 0 of 96 pairs are byte-identical; the *quietest* pair still carries 2211 pixels at ≥ 32/255 — 44× the renderer noise floor.  The zero-difference consistency gate R1-F7 asked for is therefore vacuous here (no pair to test it on), and the honest reading is the opposite of the one it was set up to check: an H-tier twin Δ > 0 does **not** require an explanation beyond ordinary visual evidence.

A high containment share therefore means *the residual is co-located with the hazard's line of sight*, not *the residual is the hazard's own pixels*.

At the noise-dominated 2/255 level the two scenes look identical (median frac 0.197 both) — that is the noise, not the scene.  At 32/255 scene14's residual is **22× larger in count** and its per-pixel magnitude is **7.6× larger**, and scene15's mean magnitude over changed pixels (4.78) is only 1.5× the renderer noise floor (3.19).

**This is the same 60/36 split that carries the twin Δ.** R2 §4.3 measured H twin Δ scene14 rgb .278/.448/.558 · depth .577–.645 versus scene15 rgb −.010/.209/−.058 · depth .016–.150.  The scene whose model response is real is the scene with a large supra-noise residual; the scene where Δ ≈ 0 is the scene whose residual is a small patch near the noise floor.  The two independent measurements agree.

## 5. What the toggle actually removes — the scope limit on every Δ in this paper

The corpus twin flips `SCENE_CONFIG['hazard_stairs']`.  In `scene14` that branch builds the shoulder massif, the side slopes, the stair, the step coursing, the parapets, the cues **and** the autumn litter; `False` replaces all of it with a flat fill (`scenes/main/scene14_grandstair_illusion.py:1885-1898`).  In `scene15` it removes the stair and the bend and extends the upper alley flat (`scenes/main/scene15_alley_labyrinth.py:2301-2304`).  The residual measured above is therefore the union of {the drop's indirect optics} ∪ {every object inside that branch}, which is exactly the qualifier R1-F12 asked for.  For contrast, the one place where the hazard alone is removed — sceneC2's `keep_dressing` arm — still moves 698078 px at ≥ 32/255.

Tier cross-check inside the same two scenes (main round, ≥ 32/255 medians): scene14 / H = 156,977 px · scene14 / none_in_fov = 83,706 px · scene14 / V = 450,238 px · scene15 / H = 7,106 px · scene15 / V = 26,152 px.
