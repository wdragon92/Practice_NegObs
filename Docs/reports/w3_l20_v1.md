### 2.1 The horizon — BS-4, and the correction the render forced

**Pre-state** `[measured, HEAD f5ab284]`: three `sc.build_building` masses —
`C(x 26…32, y ±14, h 13.0)` · `D(x −6…20, y 16…22, h 11.0)` · `E(x −40…−32, y ±16, h 16.0)` —
each a solid shell collider plus a uniform window grid, sill bands, downpipes, AC units, doors

**13.75 m**. The mass runs off the top edge of frame; there is no sky in the cut.

**Built**: the same **axis-aligned** identity — that contrast is this scene's whole subject —
rebuilt as a far silhouette through `bk.plan_building(..., kind="backdrop")`, whose contract is
*distant silhouette only, no windows, 3–4 prims*. `bk.judged_eyes(0.0)` hands the planner this
scene's real preset eye set, so `d_true` / `in_frame` / `z_ceil` come from the judging geometry
and not from `|facade plane|` (B-F3). The acceptance condition is stated as a number and
**checked at assembly time**, printed per block:

the top of frame twice. `parapet=` gets the **shell** material — a 0.72-grey cap on a dark mass at
40–48 m reads as a lit highlight along the roofline, and a silhouette has no cap by definition.

edge** while N1/S1 overhung it in `y`. The first pilot cut shows the plane's own horizon seam
running behind them. The blocks are re-solved inside the plane with ≥ 2 m margin, **containment
is now a gate** (`[selfcheck] 지면 포함 … 이탈 0건`) rather than an assumption, and GT-45 was
amended **before** the corrected geometry landed. **W1 is deleted, not re-sited**: the preset axis
is +X (spec §D) and all four mise-en-scène cuts look east, so a −X mass is behind every judged eye
and was paying 3 prims for nothing.

**Prim arithmetic**: 3 masses × (shell + window grid + sills + downpipes + AC + door + fire mark)
