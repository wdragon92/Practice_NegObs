## 3. The seven rows

### 3.1 Dry-stone 자연석 terrace walls · 5 beds · 374 blocks

Beds **B1/B2/B3** on the −Y flank at `y −9.4…−5.8` and **B4/B5** on the +Y flank at
`y 13.2…18.4`, stepping **up away from the water** at bed tops **0.50 / 1.00 / 1.50**, which is
what a 계단식 화단 on a flat lakeside terrace has to do and what G9 shows.

Each bed is one solid core (the retained planting mass, `collider=True`) plus a coursed facing on
its **exposed** faces only — declared per bed, not derived, because the derivation would be wrong:
B2 stands behind B1, so B2's `+X` face is exposed **only above B1's top** and the facing is cut to
that band. No block is built where nothing can see it.

The facing is real 자연석 건식쌓기 practice, not a texture:

| parameter | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| course height | **0.220 m** | the ordinary Korean 자연석 쌓기 course band |
| block length | **0.36–0.62 m**, drawn per block | so **no vertical joint continues** into the course above — the single cue that separates a dry-stone wall from a block wall |
| batter | **1 : 6** | the dry-laid lean that keeps a mortarless wall standing |
| block depth | **0.26 ± 0.018 m**, bedding face pinned to the wall plane | courses catch light one at a time instead of reading as one plane |
| coping (갓돌) | 0.10 m continuous band | the wall head |

**LINT-10, adjudicated by moving the code rather than by writing a paragraph.** The linter flags
any `jitter=` kwarg and asks the owning WP to classify it: §1.2 X2 keeps **size / interval
variation** and abolishes **placement jitter**. This draw is on the block's **depth** with its
bedding face fixed, so the front face moves *because the stone is a different stone*, not because
a stone was nudged — size variation. The parameter is renamed **`depth_var`**, so the
classification lives at the call site and the finding clears honestly rather than being argued
away. `placement_lint` LINT-10: 1 → 0.

### 3.2 Massed ground cover, one species per bed

`sc.place_shrubs(..., pool=[<one asset>], seed=<bed seed>)`. K4(b)·S-2 made the species a
**per-bed draw**; a one-member pool removes the draw altogether, so the bed is monospecific **by
construction** and the record is exact. Points are a jittered ~0.95 m lattice culled to `n` — a
planting bed is planted on a spacing, not scattered, but never on a visible grid.

| bed | top z | species | measured basis | target h | n |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 (nearest water) | 0.50 | `Burning_Bush` | red **0.399** — the only warm mass in the library | 0.85 | 15 |
| B2 | 1.00 | `Juniper` | `green1` yg 1.000, evergreen = season-neutral | 0.60 | 16 |
| B3 | 1.50 | `Holly` | `hollyprivet` green 0.690 / yg 0.310, evergreen | 1.05 | 14 |
| B4 | 0.50 | `Yew` | `green1`, evergreen | 0.70 | 16 |
| B5 | 1.00 | `Boxwood` | `green1`, evergreen | 0.65 | 17 |

**The seasonal audit is an instrument, not a paragraph** (§7 ruling 8: *"each scene runs an
internal seasonal audit"*; C-21: *"name heuristics are dead — the pixel rule is the only
instrument"*). `season_audit()` re-measures the hue histogram of every shrub basecolor atlas in
the library at run time and FAILs the build if a bed is massed with a banned species, or with an
asset that is not a `VEG_SHRUBS` row:

```
burningbush_leaf   red .399  or .005  yel .002  yg .262  grn .000  mag .000
forsythiaflower    red .000  or .064  yel .936  yg .000  grn .000  mag .000
green1             red .000  or .000  yel .000  yg 1.000 grn .000  mag .000
hollyprivet        red .000  or .000  yel .000  yg .310  grn .690  mag .000
lawngrass_a        red .000  or .555  yel .232  yg .177  grn .019  mag .000
rhododendron       red .046  or .003  yel .222  yg .000  grn .000  mag .729
switchgrass        red .000  or .000  yel .000  yg .000  grn 1.000 mag .000
```

Three findings it produced, all acted on:

1. **`Burning_Bush` is re-admitted, and it is the only warm mass in the library.** Its ban
   (`scene_common.py`, the `SHRUB_ORNAMENT` block) reads *"red 30.2 % (autumn colour). Fine for
   C2, but banned in summer and all-season scenes"* — the ban's **stated premise is the global
   summer lock**, and §7 ruling 8 retired that lock. On a scene pinned autumn the asset is not
   merely admissible, it is the correct one. Taken through `pool=`, never by editing
   `SHRUB_ORNAMENT` (K4's file, frozen this window).
2. **`Forsythia` stays banned even in autumn.** Yellow 0.936 on a *blossom-only* atlas is
   March–April. An autumn pin does not license a spring flower — named so the next reader does
   not "fix" the omission.
3. **The library has no flowering ground cover and no autumn tree.** `Rhododendron`'s magenta
   0.729 is the `/Root/Flowers` strip that K4(0)'s wrapper removes library-wide (**K4-F1** — the
   fix, not a regression), and every tree row measures green 74–100 % / orange 0.000 / red 0.000.
   G9's pink cosmos masses **cannot be built from stock**. §7 routes the procurement.

**S09-F1 — a K4(b) defect found while doing this, worth a routed row.**
`SHRUB_SPECIES` has seven roles, and **three of them are inert**: `verge_turf`
(`Grass_Short_A/B`), `edge_weed` (`Grass_Short_C`) and `riparian` (`Switchgrass`) name assets
that are **not rows of `VEG_SHRUBS`**. `place_shrubs` filters both `species=` and `pool=` through
`VEG_SHRUBS`, so those three roles silently fall back to the default pool and `Switchgrass` is
**unreachable through `place_shrubs` entirely** — which matters here, because an ornamental-grass
mass is one of the things G9 shows and `riparian` is the role that was supposed to deliver it.
The assets are on disk; the registry rows are missing. Owner: Lane-1 / K4.

### 3.3 Zigzag timber boardwalk

The pre-state was a 2.4 × 44.0 m plank-seamed plate at one `x` — a *floor*, not a route: it makes
no turn, it goes nowhere, and its seams ran **along** its own long axis, the one direction a plank
never runs.

```
L1  y −20.4…−18.0, x −16.0…−9.2   (east, off the park)
L2  x −11.6…−9.2,  y −20.4…−12.4  (north, along the shore)
L3  y −12.4…−10.0, x −11.6…−0.6   (east, out to the step courses)
```

Legs overlap by a full leg width at the turns, so the corners are solid and no seam opens.
Width **2.400 m** on every leg (unchanged; 조경설계기준 5.9(4)'s 1.5 m with margin for a two-way
lakeside promenade). **`top_z` stays 0.060** — the value the old deck shipped. This is a planform
and member change, **not a height change**, and that is what keeps the row's z-profile claim true.

Sections are **stocked 방부목**, on the scene10 precedent (`scene10:262-265`; 산림청고시 2014-2
제8조 fixes the 데크판재 thickness series at 21/24/27/30 mm and the width series at 90–300 mm in
10 mm steps, so a 0.145 m plank is a KCS *specification* width and not a retail size):

| member | section | count |
|---|---|---|
| plank 데크판재, laid **across** the leg, 6 mm gap | 25 × 140 @ 0.146 pitch | **175** |
| joist 장선 | 38 × 140 @ 0.45 | **18** |
| beam 멍에 | 90각 | 6 |
| post 기둥 | 120각 @ 2.70 | **26** |
| rim 마구리 | 25 × 140 | 6 |

**Why the last two metres were declined.** G9 shows timber running onto the stone. Laying treads
on the embankment would raise a walked surface by the 25 mm plank — above `GT_DELTA` 0.020 — which
is a full re-cache and a new drop-adjacent surface, bought for a dressing detail. The boardwalk
therefore ends at `x = −0.60`, 0.60 m short of the first stone riser, at `|y| 10.0…12.4`, and the
stone courses take the descent. Declined on purpose, recorded rather than quietly dropped.

## 4. The placement law, and the gate that enforces it

Every element these rows add is asserted **outside the ±30° horizontal FOV of all nine
`sc.grid_views` presets**, from the coordinates, with no render. This is the S09-C `land_posts`
argument (`scene09:291`) generalised: that row proved four posts one at a time **in a comment**,
and a comment cannot fail a build.

`fov_selfcheck()` takes each element's footprint and, for every preset eye, either finds it behind
the eye or computes the smallest `|bearing|` any point of it subtends — exactly, by taking `x` at
its maximum and `|y|` at its minimum, which is the extremum of `atan(|y|/dx)` on a box.

```
화단 B1        38.1° @eye(-10,0)   OUT (+8.1°)      보드워크 L1   87.5°  OUT (+57.5°)
화단 B2        71.0°               OUT (+41.0°)     보드워크 L2   86.3°  OUT (+56.3°)
화단 B3        behind every preset OUT              보드워크 L3   46.8°  OUT (+16.8°)
화단 B4        60.7°               OUT (+30.7°)     안내 거치대   47.0°  OUT (+17.0°)
화단 B5        81.4°               OUT (+51.4°)     수련 ×4      33.2…37.1°  OUT (+3.2…+7.1°)
⇒ OK — 판정컷 침범 0건
```

It is not decoration: it **failed** on the first lily siting (§3.5) and forced a real move.

**What the gate does not cover, stated plainly.** It checks the elements this lane *adds*. The
element this lane *deleted* — the straight deck slab — **was inside the `d10` cone**, and that is
the whole of §6.3. A gate that only looks forward is exactly how that was missed.
