Four things in that block are deliberate:

* **`p.ridge`, not a scene-local constant.** S01 had to measure the roof furniture by hand
  (`ROOF_ALLOW = 2.90`) because `building_kit`'s total-height invariant stops at the shell top
  while `build_korean_building` puts a parapet band and a penthouse above it — S01-F1. K-micro
  item 6 fixed that by making the planner **state** it (`p.roof_allow` / `p.ridge`), and KM-F6
  recorded that scene01 could now delete its constant. **This scene never grew one**: it reads
  `p.ridge` from the first line it was written. A hand-derived duplicate of a number the kit
  already publishes is a defect waiting for the kit to change.
* **All three stay inside the ±30° cone** (bearings from the d2 eye: E1 −19.8…0° · E2 +4.2…+17.0°
  · E3 +22.9…+28.5°). They are backdrop **by distance**, not by being hidden outside the frame —
  which is the difference between opening a horizon and deleting one.
* **`parapet=` gets the shell material.** At 50–56 m a 0.72-grey capping band on a stone
  silhouette reads as a lit roofline highlight; S01's first pilot shipped exactly that and had to
  fix it. A silhouette has no cap by definition.
* **Flanking wings and a −X block were costed and dropped.** N/S wings at |y| 34…44 and a mass
  behind the office facade bear **80–130° off every judged view axis** — invisible in all 13 cuts —
  and the 8.5 m facade wall already closes −X. v5.2 §6's *"emptiness is the default"* applies to
  prims nobody can see; three blocks is the whole backdrop.

E3 is the block the intake's archetype justification actually names: *"G1's large stone
institutional block at frame right is the closest real Korean referent for 21's government-office
facade."* It is the widest, the tallest and the one at frame right.

### 2.2 The civic planting — the scene's first vegetation, and a missing table row

scene21 shipped with **zero plants** on a 110 × 90 m turf plate, against a reference whose flank
carries *"clipped conifer/shrub domes, mature broadleaves in autumn colour."* Two populations,
each monospecific, physically separate — the G18 "two distinct beds" reading. Cross-cutting read 3
(*"one species per route"*) is satisfied **per population**, which is what the images actually
show; it is not a claim that a whole scene has one species.

| population | species | asset | where | count |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| route broadleaf | **`ash`** | `Trees/Fraxinus.usd`, native 5.34 m | row A `(x −14.0, −6.0) × y ±16.0` · row B `(x 38.0, 46.0) × y ±12.0`, pitch **8.0 m** = `sc.TREE_PITCH_M` | 8 |
| clipped dome | **`planter_accent`** | `Shrub/Yew.usd` (주목), the library's declared *formal planter* role | `x −13.0 / −9.5 / −6.0 × y ±12.5` | 6 |

* **`species=` is passed explicitly at every call site, and here that is not a formality.**
  `SCENE_SPECIES` (`scene_common.py:2431-2462`) carries rows for Scene01…Scene20 and **no row for
  Scene21**, so an unqualified `build_tree` in this file resolves to `SCENE_SPECIES_DEFAULT =
  "elm"` — a 3.09 m near-field street sapling that cannot read as G1's mature civic broadleaf at
  20–50 m. `ash` is the civic choice the sibling monumental-stair scene14 and the plaza scenes
  05 / 08 / 19 all carry. The missing row is **L21-F2**, filed rather than patched: kits are
  frozen this window and a `SCENE_SPECIES` edit is a kit edit.
* **Row B is the row that does work in the judged frames.** Row A sits on the terrace flank where
  G1 puts its stand, but at bearings 61–78° it is outside the ±30° preset cone and reads only in
  `oblique` and at the lateral edge of `preset_h1.8_d10`. Row B stands across the head of the
  lower plaza at **bearings 12.1–14.0° from the d10 eye, 48–57 m**, with crown tops at z **+3.65**
  against a ceiling of 7.5 — inside every +X frame, under the roofline, in front of the backdrop.

* **The judged-eye census is a gate, not a note.** scene21 has no row in `w3_md_reverts_v1.md` §5

  The south dome row was moved twice during siting for this number alone.
* **The axis stays empty.** v5.1 §21's ruling for this scene — *"dignity comes from symmetry and

### 2.3 Season — G1 is autumn **in leaf**, so the answer is dressing, not `bare=`

The seasonal audit result is that **G1 carries no bare trunk anywhere**: the frame-right maple has
a full orange crown, the mid-ground broadleaves are turning, the conifer domes are dark green.
Pinning this scene leaf-off would contradict its own reference, so `build_tree(bare=)` is **not**
used and the K4(0) wrapper route is not entered. Stated rather than skipped — "the mechanism
exists" is not a reason to fire it.

What autumn buys is built:

* **Litter — 90 instances, three regions.** `sc.scatter_debris`'s default pool is `VEG_DEBRIS`,
  which is already the `Debris/*fall*` set, so autumn needs no pool argument. The tread region
  passes a `ground_fn` that seats each instance on the tread it actually lands on and lays it
  along the local slope; without it a leaf on a 0.150 m riser floats. `edge_bias` differs per
  region because the sweeping does: 0.45 on the treads (leaves collect in the step corners, which
  is what G1 shows), 0.30 at the stair foot, 1.20 on the open terrace so the walking line stays
  clear.
* **The terrace region stops at `x = −2.0`, two metres short of the drop edge.** An exposed nosing
  line is swept clean by wind in reality, and it keeps the `GT-E2` edge guard band free of a new
  full-width cross feature — the **S01-F7 class**, avoided by construction rather than discovered
  in the round. The cost is stated in §6.5: it is also why `flat_gnd` does not improve.
* **Turf tint** `(0.55, 0.68, 0.42)` → **`(0.60, 0.63, 0.38)`** — desaturated and warmed, green
  still the largest channel. A straw-yellow lawn would be a different season, not this one.

**What autumn does not buy, and this is a real divergence:** the crowns stay green. Every tree and
shrub asset in the library is a green-foliage scan (`veg_manifest_w2`: green 74–100 %, orange
0.000, red 0.000 across all ten rows), so a leaf-**on** autumn cannot be expressed by asset choice
at all. This is the same gap §7.2 opened as *"S09 autumn/flowering procurement gap → open row; an
autumn-leaf-tint TREATMENT wrapper (T4b pattern applied to leaf materials) is the candidate
mechanism"*. scene21 is a second consumer for that row and is recorded as such (**L21-F5**), not
worked around with a tint that would also recolour the trunk.

**Time of day was deliberately NOT moved.** G1 is late-afternoon warm low sun; this scene keeps
`qwantani_noon_puresky_4k.exr` at `noon_sun_elev 49.79` / `SUN_AZ_OFFSET 206.5`. The season policy
(§7-8) asks each scene to pin its image's **season**, and that is what was done. The sun bearing
here is not a style choice: the v5 verdict solved a **foreground crush** (d10 preset column mean
RGB (23,26,28)) by moving the colonnade and wall 3.2 m west *and* turning the shadow azimuth from
25° to 60°, with the reach arithmetic written into PARAMS. Dropping the sun to a low afternoon
elevation lengthens every shadow by `1/tan(elev)` and re-opens precisely that defect on a scene
whose identity is a grazing-light illusion. A relight here needs its own round with its own
crush measurement; it is not a dressing change.

### 2.4 C6 — the bollard template, and why every post is compliant

`build_bollard_std` kept its name and call sites and now delegates to
`props_kit.build_bollard_v2`. The 7 posts at the lower-plaza north entry go from **2 prims each**
(a bare `sc.build_bollard` cylinder + a band cylinder — no cap, no flange, penetrating the slab)
to **6** (root · body · dome · plate · anchor cover · band): **14 → 42 prims**.

**The collider does not move.** `radius=0.075` and `height=0.90` are passed explicitly, so the
body collider keeps its dimensions and world position exactly; the dome, plate, cover and band are
not colliders. That is the one place this row differs from scene20's GT-45, which took the
builder's defaults and moved its bollard colliders `r 0.075 → 0.055` / `h 0.90 → 0.85`.

`compliant=True` on all seven, with the reason at the call site. The builder's `compliant=False`
arm is a *deterministic* reproduction of the measured field population (about three quarters of
installed Korean bollards miss height, spacing or band). That is a fact about an ordinary street.
This row is the **vehicle-entry control at the north edge of a government-office plaza** — the one
population that does get installed to spec — so mixing a non-compliant post in would be inventing
a defect the site would not have. Both dimensions stay inside 편의증진법 별표2 제7호
(h 0.80–1.00 · Ø 0.10–0.20), asserted in the smoke run.

Visible result: `_w3_l21_crops/l21_c6_bollard_before_after.png`. Honestly read, **the dome cap is
the only difference resolvable at any judged range**; the base plate and anchor cover are below
the pixel scale of every cut in this scene. They are built because the form is the deliverable,
not because this scene's frames will show them.

### 2.5 The rectangles ban — audited, and the finding is that there was nothing to delete

`plaza_granite`'s `surface` row is `(("crack", 4), ("stain", ("dirt", "water")))`: **GT-24 struck
`("patch", 1)`** from all three unit-paving profiles, and `apply_ground` only builds what the
profile prescribes. Measured on the composed inventory — 35 GKit prims: crack 12 · joint 6 ·
manhole 4 · gully 4 · stain 8 · wear 1, **`Patch_*` = 0**.

So scene21's `gkit=dict(..., patches=[(−1.25, 0.55), (−3.70, −0.60)])` had been emitting nothing
since GT-24 landed, and the PARAMS comment above it — *"the near window is filled by repair
patches"* — was **false**. Both are deleted: the sites because a call that builds nothing reads as
intent, the sentence because withdrawing a false claim is worth more than deleting a dead
argument. The near-window claim is now stated honestly: nothing fills it (§6.5).

One artefact of this is worth naming so nobody miscredits it. The baseline of record
`260730_w2d_fix` was rendered at HEAD `386fc88`, **before** GT-24, so its frames still contain the
repair rectangles — visible at frame left in `l21_backdrop_h1.8_d10_before_after.png`. They are
gone in the new round. **That improvement is GT-24's, not this lane's**; this lane's contribution
to it is 0 prims.

### 2.6 The one change the render refuted — G1's brick-red band

G1 puts a **salmon/rose brick-red block band** in an otherwise light-grey granite plaza, and
cross-cutting read 1 lists *"a paving band"* among the legitimate functional ground marks. The
first pilot therefore bound the two lower-plaza apron bands to `brick_red` at the **BS-1** scale
(spec §10.7 names scene21 in its 17-scene list; the rendered course at `scale_m` 2.0 is **154 mm**
against the Korean **67 mm** of KS L 4201, i.e. **2.30 ×**, so the value moved 2.0 → 1.00).

The render refuted the asset, not the intent. `brick_red` is a **wall** brick scan with a large
per-brick albedo spread; at 1.00 m tiling on a 0.40 m wide × 32 m long strip it resolves into
individual bricks, roughly half near the marble tone and half dark red, so the run reads as a
**broken chain of red rectangles** across the plaza — `_w3_l21_crops/l21_band_brick_refuted.png`.
That is the *"이상한 사각형 무늬"* the rectangles ban exists to kill, arrived at from the opposite
direction, and it would have been indefensible to ship it under a lane whose §2.5 is an audit of
exactly that vocabulary.

Reverted to `band_dark`. With the band reverted and the three brick masses gone, `brick_red` has
**no consumer left in this scene**, so the role is dropped from `ASSET_ROLES`, from `material.scale`
and from `setup_materials` entirely. **BS-1's scene21 entry is discharged by deletion of the
binding rather than by a corrected scale value** — a deleted binding satisfies a scale rule more
completely than a re-scaled one. The right asset for G1's band is a **점토블록 paving** texture,
which the library does not have; that is a procurement row (**L21-F4**), not a scene edit.

The geometry hash is **identical across this revert** (559 / `35ff369a` before and after), which
is itself a useful fact: the invariance harness records prim shape and transform, not material
bindings, so a material-only change is invisible to R-4/R-6 by construction. Anyone using the
hash as a "nothing changed" proof should know that.

### 6.4 GRAZE adjudication — three flags, all NON-DEFECT, and the method is not "I looked"

invites a comfortable reading, so it is backed by a measurement that **separates the two
hypotheses**: if a hidden drop were revealed, new structure would appear on the **walked** side of
the line; if only the background changed, the walked side is untouched.
