### 3.2 A finding worth carrying back to §10.8: `d_true` is the wrong instrument for a street wall

`bk.eye_distance` returns the distance to the **nearest point of the facade rectangle**. For a
wall that runs *past* the camera, that nearest point is the lateral standoff — **10.00 m** — and
never a point the camera can see. Consequences `[measured]`:

* every block computes `tier = "near"` (or `mid` for the two eastmost), and
* `z_ceil = 0.3 + 0.1405 · d_true` lands at **1.71 – 3.70 m**.

Backdrop policy (2) ("build nothing above `z_ceil`") would therefore cut a four-storey street wall
off at first-floor sill height. The policy is written for a building the camera **faces**, not for
one it **travels along**. The frame test agrees with that reading: the ±30° cone only reaches
|y| = 10 at X ≥ 17.3 m, so the wall enters frame at world x ≈ +7.3 (d10) / +15.3 (d2) — **only the
far run is ever seen, and only in silhouette**, which is precisely the case `kind="backdrop"` was
written for. Ruling 4's instruction to force the kind is therefore not a shortcut; it is the
correct instrument, and §10.8 should gain a sentence saying policy (2) does not apply to facades
whose nearest point is behind the eye.

### 3.3 Two rounds — the first one failed, and why

r1 put the facades on the walk edge (|y| = 8.0) with `brick_red` in the shell mix. Result against
the baseline: **FAIL 2 · WARN 10**, with **OCCL** newdark 4.7 % / blob 1.9 % (`h1.8_d2`), 3.1 %
(`h0.9_d2`), 2.3 % (`shadow_band`), **PHOTO** −16.4 mean / +13.0 pp dark on `h1.8_d2`, and
`under_canopy` dark 40.7 → 57.1 %.

Diagnosis, not guesswork — the newdark mask was localised: **row bands 0–3 (top 44 %), column
bands 0–1 and 6–7 (both edges)**, 0.0 % in the near band. That is sky → wall, not "camera
swallowed by geometry". Two independent causes:

1. **The street wall gets no direct sun, ever.** `SUN_AZ_OFFSET = 146.5` puts the shadow azimuth
   at 0°, i.e. **along +X** (that is the scene's signature — it is what throws the canopy shadow
   down the flight). A wall running along X therefore has its facade normal perpendicular to the
   sun's horizontal bearing and lives on skylight alone. With `brick_red` at a **0.087 linear
   albedo** `[measured on the map]` and r1's 0.78 tint, the worst blocks were an effective
   **0.050** — they rendered under the OCCL < 25 line.
2. **The canyon was too narrow.** Sky-view factor on a 16 m floor at h ≈ 14 is
   cos(atan(14/8)) ≈ **0.50**, and the judged subject already lives on indirect light.

r2 fixed both causes instead of shrinking the deliverable:

| | r1 | r2 |
|---|---|---|
| facade line | \|y\| = 8.0 (walk edge) | **\|y\| = 10.0** (SVF 0.50 → **0.58**) |
| tall blocks | 26.0 / 24.0 m | **22.0 / 21.0 m** |
| shell palette | `concrete_wall` · `brick_red` · `plaster` | `concrete_wall` · **`marble_light`** · `plaster` |
| effective linear albedo | 0.050 – 0.247 | **0.198 / 0.257 / 0.328** |
| verge strips | 1 apron (east only) | 3 strips (both verges + east) |

`brick_red` leaving the street-wall palette is also the more Korean answer: 근생 street walls are
tile / 석재 / 미장 far more often than face brick, and G2's own two flanks are grey tile and metal.
`brick_red` is untouched everywhere else — building C keeps it — so **BS-1's pending 2.0 → 0.90–1.10
`scale_m` sweep still lands in one place** and is not pre-empted here.

All three shells stay inside §1.11's ≤ 0.55 band and stay **textures with a tint**, never constants
(the §1.11 rule and the W2 F1 defect it exists to avoid). `const_color_audit` 137 wirings, **0
violations**.
