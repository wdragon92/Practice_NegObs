> We restrict the hidden-tier claim to the **forward, cue-accompanied family (3b)**. In the
> underfoot family (3a) the twin effect is a uniform null across all 11 camera poses × 2 scenes × 3
> heights (removing any single pose changes it by +0.002), while the V tier on the very same frames
> remains at +0.405 — so the null is a property of short-range self-occlusion, not a viewpoint
> artefact. Within 3b the causal effect is further concentrated in a single scene: the tread
> self-occlusion scene (scene14, Δ_3b = +0.441, positive on 20 of 20 poses), while the corridor
> refraction scene (scene15) is weak and, under the twin conditional, close to zero.
