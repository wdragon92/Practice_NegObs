### 4.1 F1 — the drop-edge occlusion instrument was wrong, and R03-1 was about to be blamed

Running the widened geometry through `river_view_selfcheck` **FAILED**: `meander_air` occlusion
31.8 % → **44.3 %** against a 35 % gate. Chasing it found a defect in the *instrument*, not the geometry.

The bridge occluder was **one axis-aligned box** spanning the pier feet (−3.70) to the parapet top (+0.70),
with its y-range inflated by `byw` to swallow the skewed deck. Two errors in one box — and both are the
error this same function already names and fixes for tree crowns:

> *"one solid box would falsely count the empty space under the crown as occlusion"*

* the **open space under the deck** (−3.70…−1.00, everything but 7 slender piers) counted as solid;
* the **AABB inflation** counted the empty corners either side of the skewed deck. At the v6 skew (14.75°,
  34 m deck) that was ±4.33 m; R03-1 takes the deck to 52 m and the local tangent to 23.65°, i.e.
  **±10.43 m** — the box grows from 17.7 m to 29.9 m of y, **3.4× the real 9.0 m deck width**, purely from a
  modelling shortcut.

Fixed by testing the segment **inside the rot group's own frame** (`_seg_hits_rot_box`) against the deck as
authored, plus one box per pier. **The proof that this is an instrument fix and not a gate tuned to a
result is that it moves the v6 arm too:**

| arm | `meander_air` occl | `bank_oblique` occl | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| v6 geometry × v6 instrument | 41/129 = 31.8 % | 26/121 = 21.5 % | OK |
| v6 geometry × **v7 instrument** | 39/129 = 30.2 % | **15/121 = 12.4 %** | OK |
| v7 geometry × v6 instrument | 58/131 = 44.3 % | 43/119 = 36.1 % | **FAIL** |
| v7 geometry × **v7 instrument** | 40/131 = 30.5 % | 26/119 = 21.8 % | **OK** |

Meander legibility improves markedly with the raised amplitude, on the same instrument:
`meander_air` bow **14.09 → 20.41 %** of frame half-width, `bank_oblique` **14.15 → 28.16 %** (gate ≥ 5 %).

### 4.2 GRAZE `preset_h0.3_d2` — FAIL, adjudicated **non-defect**

The tool grades it FAIL (`gz_spec 21.50` against `GRAZE_SPEC_FAIL 20.0`) and states outright that it cannot
decide (*"자동 확정 불가"*), demanding a visual read of rows y170–312/540. Adjudication, on its own recorded
metrics:

| metric | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `gz_step_b` → `gz_step_a` | 81.15 → **80.42** | the line on the drop row before and after |
| `gz_step_ratio` | **0.991** | **the drop edge is intact to 1 %** |
| `gz_row` vs `gz_haz_row` | **211** vs **248.7** | the max-change row is **38 rows off** the drop row |
| `gz_step_off` | 38 | against `GRAZE_PERSIST_ROWTOL = 4` |
| `gz_step_dom` | 3.39 | against `GRAZE_PERSIST_DOM = 3.5` |

1. **The drop-row step is intact** — ratio 0.991. The direction label *"무너졌다"* is derived from
   `ratio < 1.0` and has no headroom at a 0.9 % difference.
2. **The change is not on the drop line.** This is exactly why the tool's own secondary discrimination
   could not auto-quiet it: its persistence test requires the max-change row to sit on the drop row
   (`row_off ≤ 4`), and here it is 38 rows away *because the change is a different object* — the widened
   water band and the deleted patch, i.e. the two authorised changes. `dom 3.39` misses its own threshold
   by 0.11 for the same reason.
3. **Visual check** (`_w3_s03_crops/graze_band_h03_d2.jpg`): the shoulder line runs unbroken across the full
   frame width in **both** arms.

Verdict: **not a concealment.** The scene's declared drop evidence — water surface, far bank, tree crowns
below eye height — got *stronger*, not weaker (§1.2, +31.8 % water area on this very cut).

### 4.3 WHITE `bank_oblique` 8.4 → 22.1 pp — attributed, and handed on

1. **The river is wider but it is still not the Han.** 33.8 m against G3's ~1 km. The subtense saturates:
   pushing the far bank to s90 (71.8 m of water) buys only 18.2 % vertical against 14.2 % at s52, while the

   willing to spend the bend — the levers and the arithmetic are in §1.2/§1.3.

   over the far bank, and the longer bridge deck fills its foreground (§4.3). Re-anchoring it
   parametrically to `far_bank["x0"] + 6` restores its documented definition in one line. **Deliberately not
   done here** — it is a judging cut and R03-1 is to be met by geometry, never by a camera edit.

   Two concrete asks: a **region-restricted scatter** (so a crest can carry stone on its track and none on
   its verge), and a look at the `gravel` material's exposure at 2 m.

   the call can be re-made against the image.

   joint**; this scene's slope is grass with a textured riprap strip at the toe. A real archetype gap, an
