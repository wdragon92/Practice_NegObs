## 2. What was built

### 2.1 The flanks — a kerbed designed lawn (R01-1, the inverse of the retired GT-4)

GT-4 (`HELD`, now `RETIRED`) planned to *extend the paving to the building faces and abolish the
turf*. G1 answers the same question — 01-B, *"paving must run FULLY to edges"* — in the opposite
direction: the paving stops at a **kerbed, inhabited lawn**. §7-6 ruled the retirement and named
the replacement; the rule survives (*no undesigned turf*), the geometry inverts.

Levels, all measured against the two plaza tops that do not move:

| Surface | z | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| upper plaza | **0.000** | unchanged |
| kerb top | **0.000** | flush with the walk — S06-B 2. asks flush … +20 mm, measured **+0 mm** |
| lawn platform | **−0.150** | = the kerb's exposure, so the two are one number, not two |
| lower plaza | **−0.600** | unchanged |
| ground plate (`GroundGrass`) | **−0.630** | unchanged; reached by 3 walkable 0.160 m grass treads |

* **Kerb** — `infra_kit.build_curb_line`, x −16.0…0.0 on both flanks, face line y = ±8.20.
  16.00 m, **13 blocks per side**, `gutter=False`, `z_road=−0.150`, `walk_z=0.000`, `embed=0.55`.
  `gutter=False` is the one place this call differs from the 보차도 call S06-B writes, and it is
  deliberate: a lawn edge has no carriageway and no L-gutter, so the default 18 mm pan cross-fall
  must not be chained in and **`gt_drop` is 0.150, not 0.168**. `embed=0.55` puts the block bottom
  at −0.700, the upper-plaza slab's own underside, so there is no void behind the face.
* **Why the face line is at ±8.20 and not ±8.00.** The plaza slab ends at y = ±8.00. A kerb face
  on that line puts a 0.20 m strip of block top **coplanar** with the plaza top. Offsetting the
  face by the block width moves the body to y 8.00…8.20 — outside the slab, no coplanar face.
* **Both runs are authored p0 = x −16 → p1 = x 0**, and which side is "road" is expressed with
  `road_side` (`left` north, `right` south). Reversing the polyline for the south run — the
  obvious way to write it — mirrors the arc length and silently puts the `lod_span` window on the
  wrong half of the run. Measured: the reversed form produced 14 blocks with the 1 m rhythm ending
  at x −12; the corrected form produces 13 blocks with the rhythm covering **x −12…0**, i.e. the
  whole ground_kit decorated strip and the whole judged eye range.
* **Edge band** — one continuous 0.20 m granite band, top = lawn top, from x −20 to x 14.20 on both
  flanks and down the east end. It is authored **under** the kerb rather than beside it, so no top
  face is ever coplanar; the kerb block spans −0.700…0.000 and simply buries the band over its run.
* **Against the LOWER plaza the same band is an UP-STEP, +0.450** — GT-34 says so in as many words,
  and `plaza_selfcheck` classifies it `up_step`. An up-step mislabelled as a drop poisons the GT
  map (the GT-1 lesson, applied here without being asked).
* **A drop is removed, not added, on balance**: the flanks used to fall **0.630 m** unguarded from
  the terrace tops (0.000) to the ground plate (−0.630), and the same 0.630 m cut ran along the
  lower plaza's east end where raw turf filled x 14…24 in front of the old building C. The flank
  maximum falls **0.630 → 0.150**. The scene's largest drop is still the stair's 0.600.

### 2.2 The environment — BS-4, and the deletion that does the real work

Buildings **R** (x −18…12, y 9.5…14, h 14.0, 42 prims) and **L** (x −20…4, y −15…−10.5, h 10.0,
26 prims) stood 9.5 m and 10.5 m from the plaza centreline. That is the *"미흡"* the review names:
from an h0.3 eye a 14 m wall at 9.5 m subtends 55° of the lateral field, and no amount of
re-cladding opens it. They are **deleted**. Nothing replaces them in the near field except lawn.

Buildings **C** and **D** survive as identity — G1 does show stone institutional blocks — but are
rebuilt through `building_kit.plan_building(..., kind="backdrop")`, whose contract is *distant
silhouette only, no windows, 3–4 prims*. `bk.judged_eyes(-2.75)` hands the planner this scene's
real preset eye set, so `d_true`, `in_frame` and `z_ceil` come from the judging geometry (B-F3),
not from `|facade plane|`.

The acceptance condition for *"환경을 트인 느낌으로"* is stated as a number and **checked at
assembly time**, printed per block: the **ridge** must sit under the frame ceiling
`z = h_eye + 0.1405·d` at that block's own `d_true`, i.e. **sky above the roofline**.

```
[backdrop] E1 W  15.0 shell h  5.70 · ridge  7.97 · backdrop / far · d_true  58.00 · in_frame True  · z_ceil  8.45 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] E2 W  16.0 shell h  6.30 · ridge  8.57 · backdrop / far · d_true  62.18 · in_frame True  · z_ceil  9.04 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] E3 W  18.0 shell h  8.70 · ridge 10.97 · backdrop / far · d_true  80.00 · in_frame True  · z_ceil 11.54 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] W1 W  18.0 shell h  6.50 · ridge  8.77 · backdrop / far · d_true  64.00 · in_frame False · z_ceil  9.29 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] N1 W  16.0 shell h  6.40 · ridge  8.67 · backdrop / far · d_true  62.75 · in_frame False · z_ceil  9.12 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] S1 W  18.0 shell h  5.10 · ridge  7.37 · backdrop / far · d_true  53.59 · in_frame False · z_ceil  7.83 · sky True · prims 3
[backdrop] 6동 18 프림 · 창 0 · 지붕선 위 하늘 6/6
```

**The word `ridge` in that line is the whole story of §6.4 — the check was wrong twice before it
was right, and only pixels found it.** The first pass compared the *declared* `h` and read 1/6;
five blocks would have shipped cutting the top edge of frame. Heights were re-derived — and the
second pass then read a confident **6/6 while the render still showed a brick wall running off the
top of `h0.3_d10`**. The cause is **S01-F1** (§8): `building_kit`'s total-height invariant is *"the
top of the shell must stay at `base_z + h`"*, and `build_korean_building` puts a parapet band and a
penthouse **above** it — a measured **+2.90 m**, identical on all six blocks — which backdrop
policy (2) (*"build nothing above `z_ceil`"*) does not gate. The check now compares
`base_z + h + roof_allow`, and the predicted ridge matches the highest emitted prim to two decimals
(E1: predicted 7.97, emitted 7.97).

**Prim arithmetic**: 4 masses × (shell + window grid + parapet) = **128 prims, 60 of them windows**
→ 6 masses × 3 = **18 prims, 0 windows**.

### 2.3 Season — G1 is autumn **in leaf**, so `bare=` is wrong here

The brief asks for a seasonal audit with `bare=` for leaf-off. The audit result is that **G1 has no
bare trunk anywhere**: the frame-right maple carries a full orange crown, the mid-ground broadleaf
row is turning, and the pines are dark green. Pinning this scene leaf-off would contradict its own
target image, so `build_tree(bare=)` is **not** used and the K4(0) wrapper route is not entered.
Stated rather than skipped, because "the mechanism exists" is not a reason to fire it.

What autumn does buy is dressing, and that is built:

* **Litter** — `sc.scatter_debris` over three regions with the `Debris/*fall*` pool, **73
  instances**: treads (cover 0.030, `edge_bias` 0.45 — leaves collect at the step corners, which is
  what the wind actually does and what G1 shows), the stair-foot apron (0.022 / 0.30) and the open
  plaza (0.012 / 1.20, so the walking line stays clear). The tread scatter passes a `ground_fn`
  that seats each instance on the actual tread it lands on and lays it along the local slope;
  without it a leaf on a 0.15 m riser floats.
* **Turf tint** — `grass` 0.55/0.68/0.42 → **0.60/0.63/0.38**: desaturated and warmed, green still
  the largest channel. A straw-yellow lawn would be a different season, not this one.

### 2.4 The plaza field is emptied (01-C · the census · R01-2)

* **Planters A(−5,−6) / B(−9,6) / D(−13,2) deleted** — 33 prims. In G1 the plaza above the steps is
  bare paving and every planted element is in the lawn strip. This is also the answer to
  `w3_md_reverts_v1.md` §5: `Planter_A` was this scene's census row, AABB **1.70 m** from
  `preset_h0.3_d5` and inside 2.5 m of **seven** judged cuts — the second-widest exposure in the
  library. The row is not mitigated, it is gone. `plaza_selfcheck` now measures the worst
  judged-eye ↔ bed distance over **all 13 cuts × all beds** and asserts ≥ 2.5 m: **2.63 m**
  (`lower_lookback` ↔ `Planter_E`).
* **`entry_canopy` deleted** (R01-2) — a free-standing 4.0 × 1.4 m slab on four posts, authored as
  building R's porch. Building R is gone, G1 has no counterpart, and U-5 does not protect it
  because it is not an underground approach. The `build_canopy_unit` helper is deleted with it
  rather than left as a dead call site that reads as intent.
* **The planting moves to the lawn** as a monospecific `elm` row, `sc.TREE_PITCH_M` = 8.0 m, 4 per
  band, `trunk_h` 3.0 → target 4.80 m (over the S-4 street floor of 3.5 m).

### 2.5 Benches (01-D · CB-5) and the species declaration

* Every `yaw` is **0.0**. The v5.1 list carried −7.0…+5.0° of hand-authored jitter beside planter
  anchors that no longer exist. The plaza row now runs parallel to the kerb line at y = 7.10, with
  **irregular spacing** (5.0 / 4.5 / 3.0 m) and a **constant bearing** — that is the difference
  between a row and a grid, and it is CB-5's pilot condition for this scene verbatim
  (*"01: benches parallel to their planter faces"*).
* `placement_lint` LINT-7 falls from **7 findings to 1** — the six per-bench yaw findings are gone
  and only the scene-level `nodata` note remains.
* Two collisions were found by arithmetic while re-siting and fixed, not shipped: a streetlight at
  y 6.80 cleared the new bench body (y 6.875) by **15 mm** (pole line moved to 6.30), and the
  (−4.0, 7.2) litter bin sat **inside** bench 2's footprint (moved to (−5.60, 7.35)).
* `species="elm"` is passed **explicitly** at every `build_tree` call site. `SCENE_SPECIES
  ["Scene01"]` already resolves to `("elm", None)`, so the value is unchanged — what changes is
  that the scene is monospecific *by declaration* instead of by a lookup a later table edit could
  silently move. `belt=` is never used: Scene01's belt is `None` and inventing a second stand here
  would contradict the frozen table.

   The new lawn kerb is exactly the `kerb_lines` datum those rules want. Deliberately not written

   as a scene-side `M["curb"]` bound at `/World/Looks/Curb` (which is what earns the R10 arris via
   `LOOK_ROLE`), because a `scene_common.TEX` row is a kit edit and kits are frozen this window.
3. **The fountain has no moving water.** A still basin is what this project's vocabulary supports;
   G1's plume is not reproduced and is not claimed to be.
4. **`cue_tactile` remains False.** Untouched by design (R16-2 stair-cue-first, and §12 do-not-touch
   6). The statutory band generator stays wired and gated. See **S01-F7** before turning it on.
5. **The lawn reads as a flat green plane beyond ~15 m.** The `grass` role tiles at 1.4 m over a
   48 × 24 m plate, so at the ranges the beauty cuts use it there is no scale left in it. This is a
   library-level texture-scale limit, not a scene parameter — the same read is present in the
   pre-round and in the pre-W3 baselines. A groundcover scatter or a second turf tone would fix it
   and both are shared-layer work.
6. **The west approach has no planting.** The two tree rows run along the ±Y flanks; looking west
   from the lower plaza there is only the 0.6 m hedge, the grass bank and the distant `W1` block,
   so `lower_lookback` reads **open but sparse** where G1 reads open and full. Adding a west row
   means either shortening `west_bank` or planting on the ground plate beyond it — both are
   geometry decisions, so they are recorded here rather than slipped into a dressing commit.
